The Loop

One window. The whole workout lifecycle.

A note, a clock, a journal and a library that all speak the same plain-text language. Scroll to walk the four parts of the app.

wod.wiki — whiteboard-script playground
01 / 04 — The Editor

Write the workout. It's just Markdown.

The note view is where workouts are authored in whiteboard-script: (3 Rounds), 10 Pushups, *:30 Rest. The ```wod block compiles as you type — swap a load, add a round, and the plan updates before you lift a finger.

Rounds · rep schemes · sections · rest timers
02 / 04 — The Timer

Press Run. The script becomes the clock.

The WallClock runs your exact script — while it silently captures reps, pace and volume as you go. Keep scrolling: one tap casts the whole thing to a Chromecast, and your phone stays the remote.

Live metric capture · cast to any TV
03 / 04 — The Analytics

Clock stops. The numbers land.

The moment the timer completes — or you hit stop — splits, volume, streaks and custom metrics are written into the day's journal entry. Your history is yours: queryable, comparable, still plain text.

PRs flagged · 7-day trends · consistency heatmap
04 / 04 — Collections & Feeds

Or don't write anything at all.

Dozens of bundled collections — Games archives, the benchmark Girls, Dan John, Girevoy Sport — plus feeds that drop a programmed WOD into your journal every day. Pick one, press Run, and the whole loop above just works.

60+ collections · daily programmed feeds
