## WOD

### Traceur — Expert
Max effort every precision. If possible, precision over a gap or to a ledge.

```time
:60 EMOM
  + Precision Ladder — 1 precision the 1st minute, 2 precisions the 2nd minute, 3 precisions the 3rd minute… continue as long as you are able. Max effort every precision; use gap | ledge if possible.
```
