# WOD 12

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 2 miles | row 500m

(3)
  10 Pull Ups
  10 Push Ups
  10 Air Squats
  10 Sit Ups
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner
Perform single-unders with rounds of 100-80-60-40-20.
If you do not have a jump rope, sub tuck jumps in rounds of 30-20-10.

```time
(100-80-60-40-20)
  Single Unders
  Sit Ups
```

## Cool Down
```time
100m Backwards Quadrupedal Movement
```
