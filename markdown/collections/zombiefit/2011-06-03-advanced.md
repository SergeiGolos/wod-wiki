# WOD 409

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile
Bike 1/2 mile
Row 250m
(3)
  10 Air Squats
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Air Squats
  10 Pull Ups
```

Pull-ups may be substituted with jumping pull-ups if needed.

## WOD

### Avancée — Advanced
On the minute, for ten minutes, complete the following:

```time
(10) :60 EMOM
  + 3 Burpees
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 7 Sit Ups
```
