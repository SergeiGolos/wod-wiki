# WOD 17

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 2 miles | row 500m

(3)
  50m Quadrupedal Movement
  10 Air Squats
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Jumping Pull Ups
```

(May sub jumping pull ups with push ups if you don’t have a pull up bar.)

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner
Complete the following sets, in this order, for time:

```time
15 Push Ups
1 Air Squat
13 Push Ups
3 Air Squats
11 Push Ups
5 Air Squats
9 Push Ups
7 Air Squats
7 Push Ups
9 Air Squats
5 Push Ups
11 Air Squats
3 Push Ups
13 Air Squats
1 Push Up
15 Air Squats
```

## Cool Down
```time
(3)
  100m Forward Quadrupedal Movement
```
