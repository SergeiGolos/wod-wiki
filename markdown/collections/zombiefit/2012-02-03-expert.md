# WOD 590

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1/2 mile | row 250m

(3)
  10 Air Squats
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Air Squats
  10 Pull Ups
```

May substitute pull-ups with jumping pull-ups if needed.

## WOD

### Traceur — Expert

```time
75 Push Ups
75 Air Squats
75 Jumping Pull Ups
75 Sit Ups
```

## Cool Down
```time
(2)
  100m Sprint
```
