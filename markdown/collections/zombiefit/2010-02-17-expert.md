# WOD 92

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 2 miles | row 250m

(3)

```

## WOD

### Traceur — Expert
For time, complete 21-15-9 repetitions of: 6-Foot Precisions, Dips and Elbows to Knees. After each total round complete a 100m sprint or sprint on a bike 1/4 mile or row 125m.

```time
(21-15-9)
  6-Foot Precisions
  Dips
  Elbows to Knees
  100m Sprint | Bike Sprint 1/4 mile | Row 125m
```

## Cool Down
```time
(3)
  Plank Hold to Failure
```
