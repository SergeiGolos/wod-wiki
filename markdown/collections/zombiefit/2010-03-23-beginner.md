# WOD 115

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | row 250m | bike 2 miles

3 rounds
  25m Quadrupedal Movement
  10 Jumping Jacks
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Pull Ups | Jumping Pull Ups
  10 Air Squats
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner
On the minute, for ten minutes:

```time
(10) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 7 Sit Ups
  + 9 Jumping Jacks
  - 2 Burpees // if round not completed within the minute
```
