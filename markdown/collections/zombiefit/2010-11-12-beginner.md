# WOD 266

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1 mile | row 250m

(3)
  10 Double Unders
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Dips
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner
For time — choose ONE of the following movements and perform 100 repetitions.

```time
100 Push Ups
100 Sit Ups
100 Jumping Pull Ups
100 Air Squats
```

## Cool Down
1 x 400m sprint.
