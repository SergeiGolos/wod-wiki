## WOD

### Avancée — Advanced
Warm up the back squat prior to starting the WOD. This is a max effort exercise. If you do not have access to a rack or barbell with appropriate weight, perform 500 air squats for time as an alternative (see scaling notes below).

```time
(7)
  1 Back Squat ?lb
```

Scaling: 500 Air Squats for time (if no barbell/rack). If performing air squats, go below parallel (below 90 degrees) with full hip activation at the top — no cheating.
