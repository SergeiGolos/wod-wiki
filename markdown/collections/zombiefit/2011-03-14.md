# WOD 351

## Warm Up

```time
Run 1/4 mile | row 250m | bike 2 miles

(3)
  25m Quadrupedal Movement
  10 Push Ups
  10 Pull Ups | Jumping Pull Ups
  10 Movement You Suck At
```

e.g., if you suck at sit-ups do 15 sit-ups; if you suck at handstand push-ups, perform 10 HSPU | a progression)

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner

On the minute for 20 minutes perform 5 push ups, 5 sit ups and 5 air squats.

```time
(20) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
```

### Avancée — Advanced

Progress through four 5-minute EMOM blocks, increasing reps each block.

```time
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 10 Air Squats
```

### Traceur — Expert

Same progression as Avancée, but perform 2 burpees after each minute/round. If you cannot complete the prescribed movements within the minute, stop, complete 2 burpees, then move on to the next minute's repetitions.

```time
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 10 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
```
