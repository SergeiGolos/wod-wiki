# WOD 87

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 2 miles | row 250m

(3)
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  5 Jumping Pull Ups
  10 Push Ups

```

## Cool Down
10 handstand push ups. Concentrate on your form. If possible do them free-standing.
