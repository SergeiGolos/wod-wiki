# WOD 336

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1/2 mile | row 250m

(3)
  50m Quadrupedal Movement
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Jumping Pull Ups | Push Ups // if you don’t have a pull-up bar
```

## WOD

### Avancée — Advanced
Progressive EMOM — increase reps every 5 minutes:

```time
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 10 Air Squats
```
