# WOD 208

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1/2 mile | row 250m

(3)
  10 Air Squats
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Air Squats
  10 Pull Ups
```

May substitute pull-ups with jumping pull-ups if needed.

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner
For time, perform the following:

```time
25m Quadrupedal Movement
100m Sprint
25 Burpees
100m Sprint
25m Quadrupedal Movement
```

## Cool Down
Tabata squats.
