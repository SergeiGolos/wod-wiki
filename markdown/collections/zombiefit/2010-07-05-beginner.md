# WOD 176

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1/2 mile | row 250m

(3)
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Air Squats
  10 Pull Ups | Jumping Pull Ups
  10 Dips
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner
```time
(5)
  25m Quadrupedal Movement
  25 Jumping Jacks
  5 Five-Foot Precisions
```

## Cool Down
```time
100 Push Ups | 100 Air Squats | 100 Sit Ups | 100 Pull Ups
```

Only pick one exercise. When performing the sets, go to failure on every set.
