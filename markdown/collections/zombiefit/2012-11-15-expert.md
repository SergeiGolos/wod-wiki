## WOD

### Traceur — Expert
Same as Avancée, plus perform 2 burpees after each round. If you cannot complete all movements within the minute, stop the exercise, complete 2 burpees, then move on to the next minute’s repetitions.

```time
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 10 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
```
