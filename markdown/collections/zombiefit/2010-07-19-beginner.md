# WOD 184

## Warm Up
```time
(3)
  50 Jumping Jacks
  100m Jog
  50m Sprint
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner
```time
10:00 AMRAP
  1 Parkour Roll
  3 Push Ups
  6 Air Squats
  9 Sit Ups
```

## Cool Down
```time
50 Precision Jumps
```

If you have experience with this movement, go outside and pick a spot to do these repetitions. If not, just pick a point on a flat surface to jump to. These jumps should be at about 65% of your max broad jump.
