# WOD 412

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1/2 mile | row 250m

(3)
  10 Air Squats
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Air Squats
  10 Pull Ups | Jumping Pull Ups // may substitute with jumping pull-ups
```

## WOD

### Traceur — Expert
Max effort every precision. If possible, precision over a gap or to a ledge.

```time
:60 Intervals
  1 Precision // add 1 Precision each minute; continue until unable
```

## Cool Down
In the smallest number of sets possible, complete 50 push ups and 50 pull ups. (May substitute with chin-ups, jumping pull-ups, | L-sit pull-ups depending on ability.)
