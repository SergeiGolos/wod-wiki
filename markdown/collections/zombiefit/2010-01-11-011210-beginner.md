# WOD 308

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | Bike 1/2 mile | Row 250m
(3)
  10 Pull Ups
  10 Push Ups
  10 Air Squats
  10 Sit Ups
```

```time

(3)
  10 Squats
  10 Push Ups
  10 Double Unders / 50 Single Unders
  10 Push Ups
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner
Perform the ladder (21-15-9) for time, then a 10 minute AMRAP.

```time
(21-15-9)
  Alternating Leg Lunges
  Push Ups
  50m Quadrupedal Movement

10:00 AMRAP
  25m Quadrupedal Movement
  5 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  15 Jumping Jacks
```

## Cool Down
```time
75 Air Squats
50 Alternating Lunges
```
