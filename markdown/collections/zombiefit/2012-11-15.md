# WOD 740

## Warm Up

```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 2 miles | row 250m

(3)
  10 25m Quadrupedal Movement
  5 Foot Precisions
  10 Push Ups
  10 Double Unders
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner

```time
(20) :60 EMOM
  + 3 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
```

### Avancée — Advanced

```time
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 10 Air Squats
```

### Traceur — Expert

Same as Avancée, plus perform 2 burpees after each round. If you cannot complete all movements within the minute, stop the exercise, complete 2 burpees, then move on to the next minute’s repetitions.

```time
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 10 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
```
