# WOD 179

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1/2 mile | row 250m

(3)
  10 Air Squats
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Air Squats
  10 Pull Ups
```

Pull-ups may be substituted with jumping pull-ups if needed.

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner
```time
(5)
  50m Quadrupedal Movement
  5 Five-Foot Precisions
  10 Dips
```

## Cool Down
```time
25 Pull Ups
```

Pull-ups may be performed dead hang, kipping, jumping, | assisted.
