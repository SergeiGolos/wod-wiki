# WOD 647

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | row 250m | bike 1/2 mile

(3)
  25m Quadrupedal Movement
  10 Push Ups
  10 Pull Ups | Jumping Pull Ups
  10 Movement You Suck At
```

If the movement you "suck at" is already listed in the warm up, perform 15 repetitions of that movement instead of 10. For movements like handstand push-ups, perform a progression (e.g., pike HSPU, HSPU negatives) as appropriate.

## WOD

### Traceur — Expert
Same as Avancée, with 2 burpees after each minute's work.

```time
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 10 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
```

If you cannot complete all movements within the minute, stop the exercise, complete 2 burpees, and move on to the next minute’s repetitions.
