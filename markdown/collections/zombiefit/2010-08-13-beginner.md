# WOD 200

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | row 250m | bike 1 mile

(3)
  25m Quadrupedal Movement
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Pull Ups | Jumping Pull Ups
```

## WOD

```time
20 Push Ups
40 Jumping Jacks
20 Sit Ups
40 Jumping Jacks
20 Air Squats
40 Jumping Jacks
20 Jumping Pull Ups
Rest 1:00
200m Sprint
```
