# WOD 292

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1/2 mile | row 250m

(3)
  10 Air Squats
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Push Ups
  10 Jumping Pull Ups
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner
Make sure your knee touches the ground on every lunge.

```time
100m Lunge Walk
100m Lunge Walk Backwards
```

## Cool Down
```time
(3)
  50m Quadrupedal Movement // forward
```

You may rest in between sets.
