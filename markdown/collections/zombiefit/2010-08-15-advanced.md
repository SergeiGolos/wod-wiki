# WOD 201

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 2 miles | row 250m

(3)
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Pull Ups / 10 Jumping Pull Ups
  10 Air Squats
```

## WOD

### Avancée — Advanced
Tabata squats, Tabata push ups, Tabata sit ups, then Tabata pull ups.

```time
(8) :20 Tabata
  Air Squats
(8) :20 Tabata
  Push Ups
(8) :20 Tabata
  Sit Ups
(8) :20 Tabata
  Pull Ups
```
