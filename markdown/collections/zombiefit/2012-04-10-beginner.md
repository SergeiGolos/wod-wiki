# WOD 634

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1 mile | row 250m

(3)
  10 Double Unders
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Dips
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner
For time, perform 100 repetitions of ONE of the following movements: Push Ups, Sit Ups, Jumping Pull Ups or Air Squats.

```time
100 Push Ups | 100 Sit Ups | 100 Jumping Pull Ups | 100 Air Squats
```

## Cool Down
```time
400m Sprint
```
