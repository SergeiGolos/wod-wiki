# WOD 183

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 2 miles | row 500m

(3)
  10 Pull Ups
  10 Push Ups
  10 Parkour Squats
  10 Sit Ups
```

## WOD

### Avancée — Advanced
Lunges performed for both legs (42 total reps on the first set) and push ups. 100m quadrupedal movement between sets.

```time
(21-15-9)
  Lunges (Left Leg)
  Lunges (Right Leg)
  Push Ups
  100m Quadrupedal Movement
```

## Cool Down
In as few sets as possible, complete 75 air squats.

```time
75 Air Squats
```
