# WOD 657

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1/2 mile | row 250m

(3)
  10 Air Squats
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Air Squats
  10 Pull Ups
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner
For time, perform 100 repetitions of ONE of the following movements:

```time
100 Push Ups
100 Sit Ups
100 Jumping Pull Ups
100 Air Squats
```

## Cool Down
```time
(3)
  100m Sprint
```
