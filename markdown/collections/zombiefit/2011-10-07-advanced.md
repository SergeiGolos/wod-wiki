# WOD 507

## Warm Up
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1/2 miles | row 250m.

May substitute push-ups for jumping pull-ups if you don’t have a pull-up bar.

```time
(3)
  50m Quadrupedal Movement
  10 Air Squats
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Jumping Pull Ups
```

## WOD

### Avancée — Advanced
Pick a precision length of 85% of your max broad jump. If possible, precision over a gap or up to a ledge.

```time
:60 Intervals
  1 Precision // increase by 1 Precision each minute; continue as long as able; use as many sets each minute as needed
```

## Cool Down
Watch parkour on YouTube. Get some sleep.
