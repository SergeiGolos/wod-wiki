# WOD 401

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 2 miles | row 250m

3 rounds
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Pull Ups
  10 Air Squats
```

May substitute pull-ups with jumping pull-ups.
