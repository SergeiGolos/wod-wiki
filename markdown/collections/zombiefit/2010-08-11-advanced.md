## WOD

### Avancée — Advanced
Progressive EMOM: increase reps every 5 minutes.

```time
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats

(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats

(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats

(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 10 Air Squats
```
