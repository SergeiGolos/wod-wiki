# WOD 590

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1/2 mile | row 250m

(3)
  10 Air Squats
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Air Squats
  10 Pull Ups
```

May substitute pull-ups with jumping pull-ups if needed.

## WOD

### Avancée — Advanced
In as few sets as possible, complete:

```time
50 Push Ups
50 Air Squats
50 Jumping Pull Ups
50 Sit Ups
```

## Cool Down
```time
(2)
  100m Sprint
```
