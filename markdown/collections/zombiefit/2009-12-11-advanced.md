# WOD 41

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 2 miles | row 500m

3 rounds
  10 Air Squats
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Jumping Pull Ups
```

May substitute push ups if you don’t have a pull up bar.

## WOD

### Avancée — Advanced
```time
(5)
  50m Quadrupedal Movement
  5 Seven-Foot Precisions
  20 Dips
```

## Cool Down
```time
25 Pull Ups
```

May be dead hang, kipping, jumping, | assisted.
