# WOD 750

## Warm Up
```time
Run 1/4 mile | row 250m | bike 0.5 miles

(3)
  25m Quadrupedal Movement
  5 Burpees
  10 Sit Ups
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner
```time
10 Five-Foot Precisions
10 Jumping Jacks
10 Push Ups
10 Air Squats
10 Burpees
10 Air Squats
10 Push Ups
10 Jumping Jacks
10 Five-Foot Precisions
```

## Post
Try to complete each individual set unbroken (i.e., without stopping for rest).
