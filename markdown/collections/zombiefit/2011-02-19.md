# WOD 336

## Warm Up

```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1/2 mile | row 250m

(3)
  50m Quadrupedal Movement
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Jumping Pull Ups | Push Ups // if you don’t have a pull-up bar
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner

On the minute for 20 minutes:

```time
(20) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
```

### Avancée — Advanced

Progressive EMOM — increase reps every 5 minutes:

```time
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 10 Air Squats
```

### Traceur — Expert

Same as Avancée, plus 2 burpees after each minute:

```time
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 5 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 5 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 5 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
(5) :60 EMOM
  + 10 Push Ups
  + 10 Sit Ups
  + 10 Air Squats
  + 2 Burpees
```

If you cannot complete all movements within the minute timeframe, stop the exercise, complete 2 burpees, and move on to the next minute’s repetitions.
