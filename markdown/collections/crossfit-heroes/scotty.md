# Scotty

## Description
Honors San Antonio firefighter Scott "Scotty" Deem. An 11-minute AMRAP of 5 deadlifts, 18 wall-ball shots, and 17 burpees over the bar.

```time
11:00 AMRAP
  5 Deadlifts 315lb
  18 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
  17 Burpees Over the Bar
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 315-lb barbell, 20-lb medicine ball to a 10-foot target |
| Women | 205-lb barbell, 14-lb medicine ball to a 9-foot target |

## Profile
Scott "Scotty" Deem, 31, of San Antonio, Texas, was killed while responding to a four-alarm structure fire on May 18, 2017. Deem and his fellow firefighters were searching the building for survivors when the conditions changed; he had served with the San Antonio Fire Department for six years. First posted on crossfit.com on June 16, 2017.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Weightlifting, gymnastics, and effort
- **Time Domain:** Fixed, 11-minute AMRAP — one minute honored per year of his life
- **Primary Movements:** Deadlifts, wall-ball shots, burpees over the bar
- **Intensity:** Very high — heavy deadlifts under sprint pace

## Collectible Metrics
- **Reps:** Total rounds and reps completed
