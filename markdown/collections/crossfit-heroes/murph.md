# Murph

## Description
Honors Navy SEAL Lt. Michael Murphy, killed in Afghanistan in 2005 and awarded the Medal of Honor. A mile run, 100 pull-ups, 200 push-ups, 300 squats, and another mile run — partition the calisthenics as needed, and wear a 20-lb vest or body armor if you have one.

```time
1 Mile Run
100 Pull Ups
200 Push Ups
300 Air Squats
1 Mile Run
```

## Profile
Lt. Michael P. Murphy of Patchogue, New York, was a U.S. Navy SEAL. He was killed in action in Afghanistan on June 28, 2005, during Operation Red Wings, exposing himself to enemy fire to radio for help for his team. He posthumously received the Medal of Honor, and the Murph Challenge raises scholarship funds in his name. Murph was first posted on crossfit.com on August 18, 2005, and is now programmed worldwide each Memorial Day.

- **Intensity:** High volume endurance — pacing and partitioning decide the outcome
