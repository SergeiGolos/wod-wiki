# Zimmerman

## Description
A twenty-five-minute AMRAP of chest-to-bar pull-ups, heavy deadlifts, and handstand push-ups.

```time
25:00 AMRAP
  11 Chest-to-Bar Pullups
  2 Deadlifts 315lb
  10 Handstand Pushups
```

## Scaling

| Level | Deadlifts |
|-------|-----------|
| Men | 315 lb |
| Women | 205 lb |

## Profile
U.S. Marine Corps 1st Lt. James R. Zimmerman, 25, 2nd Battalion, 6th Marine Regiment, died November 2, 2010 conducting combat operations in Helmand Province, Afghanistan.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics and heavy barbell
- **Time Domain:** Long, 25-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Chest-to-bar pull-up, deadlift, handstand push-up
- **Intensity:** High-skill volume; handstand push-up capacity is the limiter

## Collectible Metrics

- **Reps:** Total rounds and reps completed
