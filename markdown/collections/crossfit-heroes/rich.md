## Profile
U.S. Army Staff Sgt. Richard Lee Vazquez, 28, of Seguin, Texas, died Nov. 13, 2013, of wounds sustained from an improvised explosive device in Panjwai District, Afghanistan. He joined the Army in 2004 and earned, among many awards, the Purple Heart, three Army Commendation Medals, and the Meritorious Service Medal. First posted on crossfit.com on July 4, 2017.
