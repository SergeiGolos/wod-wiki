# Sisson

## Profile

Army 2nd Lt. Justin Sisson, 23, of the 101st Airborne Division's 4th Brigade Combat Team, died June 3, 2013, from a suicide bomber's wounds in Chamkani, Afghanistan. A 20-minute AMRAP of rope climbs, burpees, and 200-meter runs, wearing a weight vest if available.

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  1 Rope Climb 15ft
  5 Burpees
  200m Run, wear a 20lb weight vest
```

## Scaling

| Level | Weight Vest |
|-------|-------|
| Men | 20 lb |
| Women | 14 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Gymnastics and monostructural cardio
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 20-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Rope climb, burpee, run
- **Intensity:** High
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (rope climbs under a vest)
