# Daniel

## Description
Honors Army Sgt. 1st Class Daniel Crabtree, killed in Iraq in 2006. A long chipper of 50 pull-ups bookending runs and two sets of 21 thrusters at 95 lb.

## Profile
Army Sgt. 1st Class Daniel Crabtree of Alden, Kansas, was a Green Beret with 1st Special Forces Group. He was killed in Al Kut, Iraq, on June 8, 2006. He is survived by his wife, Kathy, and daughter, Mallory. Daniel was first posted on crossfit.com on June 15, 2006.
