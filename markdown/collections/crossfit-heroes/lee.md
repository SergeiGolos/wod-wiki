# Lee

## Description
Five rounds pairing a 400-meter run with a 1-3-5 barbell complex, three muscle-ups, and a rope climb.

```time
(5)
  400m Run
  1 Deadlift 345lb
  3 Squat Cleans 185lb
  5 Push Jerks 185lb
  3 Muscle Ups
  1 Rope Climb 15ft
```

## Profile
U.S. Army Staff Sgt. Dick Alson Lee Jr., 31, of Orange Park, Florida, was assigned to the 95th Military Police Battalion, 18th Military Police Brigade, 21st Theater Sustainment Command, based in Sembach, Germany. He died on April 26, 2012, from injuries sustained when his vehicle encountered an improvised explosive device in Afghanistan. He is survived by his wife, Katherine; his sons, Joshua and David; his mother, Brenda, and her husband, Larry Carroll; his father, Dick Sr.; his sister, Specialist Vanessa Compton; and his brother, Michael Carroll. Lee was first posted on crossfit.com on Nov. 16, 2013.
