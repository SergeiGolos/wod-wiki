# Miron

## Description
Honors Polish Army officer Mirosław "Miron" Łucki. Five rounds for time of an 800-meter run, 23 back squats at three-quarters body weight, and 13 deadlifts at 1.5 body weight.

```time
(5)
  800m Run
  23 Back Squats 0.75bw
  13 Deadlifts 1.5bw
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | Back squats ¾ body weight, deadlifts 1½ body weight |
| Women | Back squats ¾ body weight, deadlifts 1½ body weight |

## Profile
Mirosław "Miron" Łucki, 38, died Aug. 23, 2013, in Ghazni Province, Afghanistan, from fatal wounds inflicted by an improvised explosive device. A warrant officer who began his service in the Polish Army in 1997, he was posthumously promoted to sergeant major. First posted on crossfit.com on April 30, 2017.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Monostructural and heavy barbell work
- **Time Domain:** Long — 4,000 meters of running and 180 squat reps
- **Primary Movements:** 800-meter runs, back squats, deadlifts
- **Intensity:** Very high — heavy squatting between runs
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (relative-strength loads)

## Collectible Metrics
