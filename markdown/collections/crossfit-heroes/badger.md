# Badger

## Description
Honors Navy Chief Petty Officer Mark Carter, a SEAL killed in Iraq in 2007. Three rounds of 30 squat cleans at 95 lb, 30 pull-ups, and an 800-meter run.

```time
(3)
  30 Squat Cleans 95lb
  30 Pull Ups
  800m Run
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | Squat cleans 95 lb |
| Women | Squat cleans 65 lb |

## Profile
Navy Chief Petty Officer Mark Carter, 27, of Fallbrook, California, was a U.S. Navy SEAL. He was killed during combat operations in Iraq on December 11, 2007. He is survived by his father, Tom; mother, Cindy; three brothers; and four sisters. Badger was first posted on crossfit.com on December 19, 2007.
