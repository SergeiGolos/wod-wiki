# JT

## Description
Honors Navy Petty Officer 1st Class Jeff Taylor, a SEAL killed in Afghanistan in 2005. A 21-15-9 triplet of handstand push-ups, ring dips, and push-ups that punishes pressing endurance with no barbell and no legs.

```time
(21-15-9) Handstand Push Ups / Ring Dips / Push Ups
```

## Profile
Petty Officer 1st Class Jeff Taylor, 30, of Midway, West Virginia, was a U.S. Navy SEAL. He was killed on June 28, 2005, while conducting combat operations near Asadabad in Kunar Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his father, John; mother, Carrie; and wife, Erin. JT was first posted on crossfit.com on July 6, 2005.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics pressing
- **Time Domain:** Short, typically 5-10 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — triceps and shoulders fail fast as the pressing volume accumulates

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
