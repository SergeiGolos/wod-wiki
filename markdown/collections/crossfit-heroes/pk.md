# PK

## Profile

U.S. Army Capt. Andrew Pedersen-Keel, 28, of the 3rd Special Forces Group at Fort Bragg, North Carolina, died March 11, 2013, from small-arms fire in Jalrez District, Afghanistan. Five rounds of back squats, deadlifts, and a 400-meter sprint, resting 2 minutes between rounds.

```time
(5)
  10 Back Squats 225lb
  10 Deadlifts 275lb
  400m Sprint
  Rest 2:00 between rounds
```

## Scaling

| Level | Back Squat | Deadlift |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Men | 225 lb | 275 lb |
| Women | 155 lb | 185 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting and monostructural cardio
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, roughly 20 minutes with rest
- **Primary Movements:** Back squat, deadlift, sprint
- **Intensity:** High per round; heavy lifting before each sprint

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
