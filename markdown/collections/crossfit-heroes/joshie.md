# Joshie

## Description
Honors Army Staff Sgt. Joshua Whitaker, killed in Afghanistan in 2007. Three rounds of 21 single-arm dumbbell snatches per arm at 40 lb and 21 L pull-ups.

```time
(3)
  21 Right Arm DB Snatches 40lb
  21 L Pull Ups
  21 Left Arm DB Snatches 40lb
  21 L Pull Ups
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | Dumbbell snatches 40 lb |
| Women | Dumbbell snatches 25 lb |

## Profile
Army Staff Sgt. Joshua Whitaker, 23, of Long Beach, California, died of wounds sustained in small-arms fire in Afghanistan on May 15, 2007. He is survived by his mother, Catherine; uncle, Robb; father, Frank Dougherty; aunt, Debra; and cousins, Rachel and Laura. Joshie was first posted on crossfit.com on December 22, 2007.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Dumbbell power plus midline-heavy gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, typically 10-20 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — the L pull-up volume punishes a fatigued midline and grip

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
