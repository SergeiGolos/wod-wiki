# Danny

## Description
Honors Oakland SWAT Sgt. Daniel Sakai, killed in the line of duty in 2009. A 20-minute AMRAP of 30 box jumps, 20 push presses at 115 lb, and 30 pull-ups.

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  30 Box Jumps 24in
  20 Push Presses 115lb
  30 Pull Ups
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 115-lb barbell, 24-inch box |
| Women | 75-lb barbell, 20-inch box |

## Profile
Oakland SWAT Sgt. Daniel Sakai, 35, was killed in the line of duty on March 21, 2009, along with fellow officers Sgt. Ervin Romans, Sgt. Mark Dunakin, and Officer John Hege. He is survived by his wife, Jenni, and daughter, Jojiye. Danny was first posted on crossfit.com on April 16, 2009.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics and barbell conditioning
- **Time Domain:** Fixed, 20-minute AMRAP
- **Intensity:** High — big sets of 30/20/30 reward sustained pacing and grip management

## Collectible Metrics
- **Reps:** Total rounds and reps completed
