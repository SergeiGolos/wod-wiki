# Santiago

## Description
Seven rounds of dumbbell hang squat cleans and pull-ups, then power cleans and handstand push-ups.

```time
(7)
  18 Dumbbell Hang Squat Cleans 35lb
  18 Pullups
  10 Power Cleans 135lb
  10 Handstand Pushups
```

## Scaling

| Level | Dumbbells | Barbell |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Men | 35 lb | 135 lb |
| Women | 20 lb | 95 lb |

## Profile
U.S. Army Sgt. Anibal Santiago, 37, 3rd Battalion, 75th Ranger Regiment, died July 18, 2010 in Bagram, Afghanistan.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Dumbbell and barbell work with gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Long, 30-45 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Dumbbell hang squat clean, pull-up, power clean, handstand push-up
- **Intensity:** Grindy; 126 dumbbell cleans accumulate before the barbell work

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all seven rounds
