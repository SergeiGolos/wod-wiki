# Hall

## Profile

Air Force Capt. Ryan P. Hall, 30, of the 319th Special Operations Squadron at Hurlburt Field, Florida, died February 18, 2012, when his U-28 aircraft crashed near Camp Lemonnier, Djibouti. Five rounds of cleans, sprints, and kettlebell snatches with 2 minutes of rest between rounds.

```time
(5)
  3 Cleans 225lb
  200m Sprint
  20 Kettlebell Snatches 53lb
  Rest 2:00 between rounds
```

## Scaling

| Level | Clean | Kettlebell |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Men | 225 lb | 53 lb |
| Women | 155 lb | 35 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting and monostructural cardio
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, roughly 15-20 minutes with rest
- **Primary Movements:** Clean, sprint, kettlebell snatch
- **Intensity:** High per round; rest resets each effort

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
