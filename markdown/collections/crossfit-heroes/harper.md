# Harper

## Profile

Firefighter Brad Harper, 23, a two-year veteran of the Phoenix Fire Department assigned to Rescue 21, died May 19, 2013, while operating at a two-alarm fire. A 23-minute AMRAP of chest-to-bar pull-ups, power cleans, squats, and 400-meter plate-carry runs.

```time
23:00 AMRAP
  9 Chest-to-Bar Pullups
  15 Power Cleans 135lb
  21 Squats
  400m Run with 45lb plate
```

## Scaling

| Level | Power Clean | Plate |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Men | 135 lb | 45 lb |
| Women | 95 lb | 25 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Gymnastics, weightlifting, and monostructural cardio
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 23-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Chest-to-bar pull-up, power clean, squat, plate run
- **Intensity:** High
