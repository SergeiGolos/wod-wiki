# Dork

## Description

Six rounds of double-unders, heavy kettlebell swings, and burpees.

```time
(6)
  60 Double Unders
  30 KB Swings 53lb
  15 Burpees
```

## Scaling

| Level | Kettlebell |
|-------|------------|
| Men | 53 lb |
| Women | 35 lb |

## Profile

Boston firefighter Michael Kennedy, 33, a Marine Corps sergeant and CrossFit coach at several Boston-area affiliates, died fighting a nine-alarm fire on March 26, 2014.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Metabolic conditioning
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, roughly 15-25 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Double-under, kettlebell swing, burpee
- **Intensity:** High, repeat sprint effort
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate (double-under volume)

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all six rounds
