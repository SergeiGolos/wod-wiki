# Carse

## Description
A descending 21-18-15-12-9-6-3 ladder of squat cleans, double-unders, deadlifts, and box jumps, each round opened with a 50-meter bear crawl.

```time
(21-18-15-12-9-6-3)
  Bear Crawl 50m
  Squat Cleans 135lb
  Double Unders
  Deadlifts 185lb
  Box Jumps 24in
```

## Scaling

| Level | Squat Cleans | Deadlifts | Box |
|-------|--------------|-----------|-----|
| Men | 135 lb | 185 lb | 24 in |
| Women | 95 lb | 125 lb | 20 in |

## Profile
U.S. Army Cpl. Nathan B. Carse, 32, 2nd Engineer Battalion, died February 8, 2011 in Kandahar, Afghanistan, of wounds from an insurgent IED attack on his unit.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Barbell, gymnastics, and odd-object crawling
- **Time Domain:** Long, 25-40 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Bear crawl, squat clean, double-under, deadlift, box jump
- **Intensity:** Grindy; the crawl fatigates the arms before every clean set

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to finish the ladder
