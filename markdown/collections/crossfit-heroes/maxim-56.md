# Maxim 56

## Description

A partner workout whose 56-second buy-in and 56-rep movements honor two Apache pilots, finished with a 5,600-meter run.

```time
For time, with a partner:
56s Handstand Hold
56 Burpees
56 Flutter Kicks
56 Walking Lunges
56 Hand Release Pushups
56 Air Squats
5600m Run
// buy-in: each partner completes a 56-second handstand hold or wall sit
// each athlete completes the 56-rep movements; sub wall sit for the hold
```

## Scaling

| Level | Buy-In |
|-------|--------|
| Men | 56s handstand hold or wall sit |
| Women | 56s handstand hold or wall sit |

## Profile

Chief Warrant Officer 2 Kirk Fuchigami, 25, of Hilo, Hawaii, and Chief Warrant Officer 2 David Knadle, 33, of Fort Worth, Texas, decorated Apache pilots with the 1st Battalion, 227th Aviation Regiment at Fort Hood, died Nov. 20, 2019, when their helicopter crashed while providing security for ground troops in Afghanistan's Logar province.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Gymnastics and metabolic conditioning
- **Time Domain:** Long, 40-60 minutes including the run
- **Primary Movements:** Handstand hold, burpee, flutter kick, walking lunge, hand-release push-up, air squat, run
- **Intensity:** Sustained moderate
- **Skill Level:** Beginner (handstand hold optional)

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
