# Desforges

## Description
Five rounds of deadlifts, pull-ups, clean and jerks, and knees-to-elbows.

```time
(5)
  12 Deadlifts 225lb
  20 Pullups
  12 Clean and Jerks 135lb
  20 Knees to Elbows
```

## Scaling

| Level | Deadlifts | Clean and Jerks |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| Men | 225 lb | 135 lb |
| Women | 155 lb | 95 lb |

## Profile
U.S. Marine Corps Sgt. Joshua Desforges, 23, 1st Battalion, 6th Marine Regiment, killed May 12, 2010 while supporting combat operations in Helmand Province, Afghanistan.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Barbell and gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Long, 20-35 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Deadlift, pull-up, clean and jerk, knees-to-elbows
- **Intensity:** Grindy; grip and posterior chain fatigue accumulate

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all five rounds
