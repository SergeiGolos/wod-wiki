# Jack

## Description
Honors Army Staff Sgt. Jack M. Martin III, Special Forces soldier killed by an IED in Jolo Island, Philippines, in 2009. A 20-minute AMRAP of push presses, kettlebell swings, and box jumps.

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  10 Push Presses 115lb
  10 KB Swings 53lb
  10 Box Jumps 24in
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 115-lb barbell, 53-lb kettlebell, 24-inch box |
| Women | 75-lb barbell, 35-lb kettlebell, 20-inch box |

## Profile
Army Staff Sgt. Jack M. Martin III, 26, of Bethany, Oklahoma, served with the 3rd Battalion, 1st Special Forces Group, at Fort Lewis, Washington. He died on September 29, 2009, in Jolo Island, Philippines, from the detonation of an improvised explosive device. He is survived by his wife, Ashley; his parents, Jack and Cheryl; and siblings Abe, Mandi, Amber, and Abi. Jack was first posted on crossfit.com on September 29, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Pressing, hinging, and jumping
- **Time Domain:** Fixed, 20-minute AMRAP
- **Intensity:** Moderate round cost — the AMRAP rewards unbroken sets and fast cycling

## Collectible Metrics
- **Reps:** Total rounds and reps completed
