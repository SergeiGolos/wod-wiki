# Santora

## Description
Three rounds scored by reps, each a minute of squat cleans, 20-foot shuttle sprints, deadlifts, burpees, and jerks with a minute of rest.

```time
(3)
  1:00 Squat Cleans 155lb
  1:00 Shuttle Sprints 20ft
  1:00 Deadlifts 245lb
  1:00 Burpees
  1:00 Jerks 155lb
  1:00 Rest
```

## Scaling

| Level | Squat Cleans | Deadlifts | Jerks |
|-------|--------------|-----------|-------|
| Men | 155 lb | 245 lb | 155 lb |
| Women | 105 lb | 165 lb | 105 lb |

## Profile
U.S. Army Sgt. Jason A. Santora, 25, 3rd Battalion, 75th Ranger Regiment, died April 23, 2010 in Logar Province, Afghanistan, of wounds sustained during a firefight with insurgents.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Barbell intervals with sprinting
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 18 minutes of work and rest
- **Primary Movements:** Squat clean, 20-foot shuttle sprint (down and back is 1 rep), deadlift, burpee, jerk
- **Intensity:** Max-effort intervals; rep total is the score

## Collectible Metrics

- **Reps:** Total reps across all intervals
