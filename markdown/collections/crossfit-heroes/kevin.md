# Kevin

## Description
Three rounds of deadlifts, hanging hip touches, and an 800-meter running farmers carry.

```time
(3)
  32 Deadlifts 185lb
  32 Hanging Hip Touches
  800m Farmers Carry 15lb
```

## Profile
U.S. Navy Special Warfare Operator 1st Class Kevin R. Ebbert, 32, of Arcata, California, was assigned to an East Coast-based Naval Special Warfare unit in Virginia Beach, Virginia. He died on Nov. 24, 2012, in Uruzgan Province, Afghanistan, while supporting combat stability operations. He is survived by his wife, Ursula; his mother, Charlie Jordan; his sister, Samantha Ebbert Martinez; his stepsisters, Amy Funk and Kate Renner; and his stepfather, Mark Ritz. Kevin was first posted on crossfit.com on Aug. 24, 2014.
