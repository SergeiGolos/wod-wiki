# Coffland

## Description
Honors Army Spc. Christopher J. Coffland. Hang from a pull-up bar for 6 minutes; every drop from the bar costs an 800-meter run and 30 push-ups before the next attempt.

```time
6:00 Hang from Pull-up Bar
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | Body weight |
| Women | Body weight |

## Profile
U.S. Army Spc. Christopher J. Coffland, 43, of Baltimore, Maryland, died Nov. 13, 2009, in Wardak Province, Afghanistan, of wounds suffered when enemy forces attacked his unit with an improvised explosive device. He enlisted one month before the Army's enlistment age limit of 42 and was assigned to the 323rd Military Intelligence Battalion. First posted on crossfit.com on November 13, 2017.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Isometric gymnastics hold with monostructural penalty
- **Time Domain:** Fixed — 6 minutes of hanging plus penalty runs
- **Primary Movements:** Bar hang, 800-meter runs, push-ups
- **Intensity:** High — grip endurance decides everything

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total elapsed time including penalty runs
