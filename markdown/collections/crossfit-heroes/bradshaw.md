# Bradshaw

## Description
Ten rounds of handstand push-ups, deadlifts, pull-ups, and double-unders.

```time
(10)
  3 Handstand Pushups
  6 Deadlifts 225lb
  12 Pullups
  24 Double Unders
```

## Scaling

| Level | Deadlifts |
|-------|-----------|
| Men | 225 lb |
| Women | 155 lb |

## Profile
U.S. Army 1st Lt. Brian Bradshaw, 24, 1st Battalion, 501st Parachute Infantry Regiment, died June 25, 2009 in Kheyl, Afghanistan, from wounds when a roadside bomb detonated near his vehicle.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics and barbell
- **Time Domain:** Long, 25-40 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Handstand push-up, deadlift, pull-up, double-under
- **Intensity:** High-volume; grip and shoulder endurance decide the pace

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all ten rounds
