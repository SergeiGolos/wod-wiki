# Coffey

## Description
Descending sets of back squats and bench presses stitched together by four 800-meter runs, finishing with a single muscle-up.

```time
800m Run
50 Back Squats 135lb
50 Bench Presses 135lb
800m Run
35 Back Squats 135lb
35 Bench Presses 135lb
800m Run
20 Back Squats 135lb
20 Bench Presses 135lb
800m Run
1 Muscle Up
```

## Profile
U.S. Marine Cpl. Keaton G. Coffey, 22, of Boring, Oregon, was assigned to the 1st Law Enforcement Battalion, 1st Marine Headquarters Group, 1st Marine Expeditionary Force, based at Camp Pendleton, California. He was killed on May 24, 2012, while conducting combat operations in Helmand Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his fiancée, Brittany Dygert, and his parents, Grant and Inger. Coffey was first posted on crossfit.com on Dec. 11, 2013.
