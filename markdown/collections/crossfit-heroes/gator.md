# Gator

## Description
Eight rounds of 5 front squats and 26 ring push-ups.

```time
(8)
  5 Front Squats 185lb
  26 Ring Pushups
```

## Scaling

| Level | Front Squats |
|-------|--------------|
| Men | 185 lb |
| Women | 125 lb |

## Profile
U.S. Army Spc. Christopher "Gator" Gathercole, 21, 2nd Battalion, 75th Ranger Regiment, killed by enemy fire May 26, 2008 in Ghazni, Afghanistan.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Barbell strength and gymnastics endurance
- **Time Domain:** Long, 25-40 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Front squat, ring push-up
- **Intensity:** Grindy; ring push-up volume accumulates quickly

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all eight rounds
