# Nate

## Description
Honors Navy Chief Special Warfare Operator (SEAL) Nate Hardy, killed in Iraq in 2008. A 20-minute AMRAP of 2 muscle-ups, 4 handstand push-ups, and 8 kettlebell swings at 70 lb.

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  2 Muscle Ups
  4 Handstand Push Ups
  8 KB Swings 70lb
```

| Sex | Loads |

| Women | 53-lb kettlebell |

## Profile
Navy Chief Special Warfare Operator (SEAL) Nate Hardy was killed on February 4, 2008, during combat operations in Iraq. He is survived by his wife, Mindi, and infant son, Parker. Nate was first posted on crossfit.com on February 12, 2008.
