# Roney

## Description
Four rounds of three 200-meter runs separated by 11 thrusters, push presses, and bench presses.

```time
(4)
  200m Run
  11 Thrusters 135lb
  200m Run
  11 Push Presses 135lb
  200m Run
  11 Bench Presses 135lb
```

## Profile
Police Service of Northern Ireland Constable Ronan Kerr, 25, of Omagh, Northern Ireland, was killed on April 2, 2011, by a car bomb outside his home in Omagh. He is survived by his mother, Nuala; his brothers, Cathair and Aaron; and his sister, Dairine. Roney was first posted on crossfit.com on Sept. 3, 2013.
