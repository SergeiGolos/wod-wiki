# Randy

## Description
Honors LAPD SWAT officer Randy Simmons, killed in the line of duty in 2008. A sprint of 75 power snatches at 75 lb for time.

```time
75 Power Snatches 75lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |

| Men | Power snatches 75 lb |
| Women | Power snatches 55 lb |

## Profile
Randy Simmons, 51, was a 27-year veteran of the Los Angeles Police Department and member of its SWAT team. He was killed on February 7, 2008, in the line of duty during a standoff. He is survived by his wife, Lisa; son, Matthew; and daughter, Gabrielle. Randy was first posted on crossfit.com on February 13, 2008.
