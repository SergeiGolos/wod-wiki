# Dobogai

## Description
Seven rounds of eight muscle-ups and a 22-yard farmers carry.

```time
(7)
  8 Muscle Ups
  22yd Farmers Carry 50lb
```

## Profile
U.S. Army Capt. Derek A. Dobogai, 26, of Fond du Lac, Wisconsin, was assigned to the 2nd Battalion, 35th Infantry Regiment, 3rd Infantry Brigade Combat Team, 25th Infantry Division, based at Schofield Barracks, Hawaii. He died on Aug. 22, 2007, in Multaka, Iraq, of injuries suffered when his unit's helicopter crashed. He is survived by his parents, David and Lisa, and his brothers, Daniel and David Jr. Dobogai was first posted on crossfit.com on Aug. 22, 2013.
