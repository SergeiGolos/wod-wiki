# Brian

## Description
Three rounds of rope climbs and heavy back squats.

```time
(3)
  5 Rope Climbs 15ft
  25 Back Squats 185lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Back Squats |
|-------|-------------|
| Men | 185 lb |
| Women | 125 lb |

## Profile
U.S. Navy Chief Petty Officer (SEAL) Brian R. Bill, 31, Naval Special Warfare, died August 6, 2011 in a helicopter crash in Wardak Province, Afghanistan.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics and heavy barbell
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 15-25 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Rope climb, back squat
- **Intensity:** High; grip fatigue from the climbs meets unbroken squat volume

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all three rounds
