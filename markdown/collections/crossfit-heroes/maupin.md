# Maupin

## Description
Honors Army Staff Sgt. Keith "Matt" Maupin. Four rounds for time of an 800-meter run and 49 reps each of push-ups, sit-ups, and squats.

```time
(4)
  800m Run
  49 Pushups
  49 Situps
  49 Squats
```

## Profile
U.S. Army Staff Sgt. Keith "Matt" Maupin, 24, of Batavia, Ohio, disappeared on April 9, 2004, when insurgents attacked his convoy with small-arms fire and rocket-propelled grenades south of Baghdad. Serving with the 724th Transportation Company, he was captured, and his remains were found on March 20, 2008. First posted on crossfit.com on April 10, 2016.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Monostructural endurance and bodyweight gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Long — sustained pace across 4 rounds
- **Primary Movements:** Running, push-ups, sit-ups, squats
- **Intensity:** High — 49-rep sets grind under fatigue

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
