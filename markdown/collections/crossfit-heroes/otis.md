# Otis

## Description

A 15-minute ascending triplet of back squats, shoulder presses, and deadlifts, adding one rep to each movement every round.

```time
15:00 AMRAP
(1-2-3-Etc)
  Back Squats 1.5x Bodyweight
  Shoulder Presses 0.75x Bodyweight
  Deadlifts 1.5x Bodyweight
```

## Scaling

| Level | Squat / Deadlift | Press |
|-------|------------------|-------|
| Men | 1.5x bodyweight | 0.75x bodyweight |
| Women | 1.5x bodyweight | 0.75x bodyweight |

## Profile

Marine Lt. Col. Christopher "Otis" Raible, 40, commanding officer of Marine Attack Squadron 211, was killed by insurgents during the attack on Camp Bastion, Afghanistan, on Sept. 14, 2012.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Short-moderate, 15-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Back squat, shoulder press, deadlift
- **Intensity:** Heavy and grinding
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate (barbell endurance)

## Collectible Metrics

- **Reps:** Rounds and reps completed
