# Riley

## Description
1.5 miles of running sandwiching 150 burpees; wear a weight vest or body armor if you have one.

```time
1.5mile Run
150 Burpees
1.5mile Run
```

## Profile
Army Sgt. 1st Class Riley G. Stephens, 39, of Tolar, Texas, was assigned to the 1st Battalion, 3rd Special Forces Group (Airborne). He died on Sept. 28, 2012, in Wardak, Afghanistan, of wounds caused by enemy small-arms fire. He is survived by his wife, Tiffany; three children, Austin, Morgan, and Rylee Ann; his parents, Michael and Joann; and his brother, Ken. Riley was first posted on crossfit.com on Sept. 28, 2014.

- **Modality:** Monostructural and calisthenics
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 30-45 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Running, burpees
