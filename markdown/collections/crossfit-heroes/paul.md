# Paul

## Description
Honors Pittsburgh police officer Paul Sciullo II, killed in the line of duty in 2009. Five rounds of 50 double-unders, 35 knees-to-elbows, and a 20-yard overhead walk at 185 lb.

```time
(5)
  50 Double Unders
  35 Knees to Elbows
  20yd Overhead Walk 185lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | Overhead walk 185 lb |
| Women | Overhead walk 125 lb |

## Profile
Pittsburgh Police Officer Paul John Rizzo Domenic Sciullo II, 36, was shot and killed in the line of duty on April 4, 2009, while responding to a domestic disturbance call. He is survived by his fiancee, Lisa Esposito. Paul was first posted on crossfit.com on April 24, 2010.
