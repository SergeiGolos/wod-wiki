# DG

## Description
A 10-minute AMRAP of toes-to-bars, dumbbell thrusters, and walking lunges.

```time
10:00 AMRAP
  8 Toes To Bars
  8 Dumbbell Thrusters 35lb
  12 Dumbbell Walking Lunges 35lb
```

## Profile
U.S. Air Force Maj. Walter David Gray, 38, of Conyers, Georgia, was assigned to the 13th Air Support Operations Squadron, based at Fort Carson, Colorado. He died on Aug. 8, 2012, from injuries suffered during a suicide-bomb attack in Kunar Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his wife, Heather; his daughters, Nyah and Ava; and his son, Garrett. DG was first posted on crossfit.com on Dec. 26, 2013.
