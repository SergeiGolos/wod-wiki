# Tama

## Description

A single-arm farmers-carry chipper broken by 31-rep sets of toes-to-bars, push-ups, front squats, and hang power cleans.

```time
For time:
800m Single-Arm Farmers Carry 45lb
31 Toes-to-Bars
31 Pushups
31 Front Squats 95lb
400m Single-Arm Farmers Carry 45lb
31 Toes-to-Bars
31 Pushups
31 Hang Power Cleans 135lb
200m Single-Arm Farmers Carry 45lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Farmers Carry | Front Squat | Hang Power Clean |
|-------|---------------|-------------|------------------|
| Men | 45-95 lb | 95 lb | 135 lb |
| Women | 35-65 lb | 65 lb | 95 lb |

## Profile

Cpl. Luke Tamatea, 31, of Kawerau, New Zealand, a 1st Battalion Royal New Zealand Infantry Regiment soldier on his second Afghanistan tour, was killed by a roadside bomb in Bamyan Province on Aug. 19, 2012.
