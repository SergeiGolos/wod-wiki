# Hotshots 19

## Description
Six rounds of squats, 19 power cleans, strict pull-ups, and a 400-meter run.

```time
(6)
  30 Squats
  19 Power Cleans 135lb
  7 Strict Pull Ups
  400m Run
```

## Profile
This workout honors the 19 members of the Granite Mountain Hotshots, a Prescott, Arizona, wildland firefighting crew overrun by the Yarnell Hill Fire on June 30, 2013. Nineteen of the crew's 20 firefighters were lost that day; only the crew lookout survived. Hotshots 19 was first posted on crossfit.com on Aug. 31, 2013, and remains a staple of firefighter memorials nationwide.
