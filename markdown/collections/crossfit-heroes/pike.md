# Pike

## Profile

Navy Chief Petty Officer Christian Michael Pike, 31, a Naval Special Warfare cryptologic technician, died March 12, 2013, in Landstuhl, Germany, of wounds sustained in Maiwand District, Afghanistan, and was posthumously awarded the Bronze Star with Valor. Five rounds of pressing: thrusters, strict ring dips, push-ups, strict handstand push-ups, and a bear crawl.

```time
(5)
  20 Thrusters 75lb
  10 Strict Ring Dips
  20 Pushups
  10 Strict Handstand Pushups
  50m Bear Crawl
```
