# Hansen

## Description
Honors Marine Staff Sgt. Daniel Hansen, killed by an IED in Afghanistan in 2009. Five rounds of 30 kettlebell swings at 70 lb, 30 burpees, and 30 GHD sit-ups for time.

```time
(5)
  30 KB Swings 70lb
  30 Burpees
  30 GHD Sit Ups
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 70-lb kettlebell |
| Women | 53-lb kettlebell |

## Profile
Marine Staff Sgt. Daniel Hansen died on February 14, 2009, in Farah Province, Afghanistan, when an IED he was working on detonated. He is survived by his mother, Sheryll; father, Delbert; sister, Katie; and twin brother, Matthew, who also serves as a Marine. Hansen was first posted on crossfit.com on May 2, 2009.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Kettlebell, burpees, midline
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 20-35 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — three movements at 30 reps each crush the trunk before it recovers

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
