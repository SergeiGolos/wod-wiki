# Coe

## Description
Honors Army Sgt. Keith Adam Coe, killed by an explosive device attack in Khalis, Iraq, in 2010. Ten rounds of 10 thrusters and 10 ring push-ups for time.

```time
(10)
  10 Thrusters 95lb
  10 Ring Push Ups
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 95-lb barbell |
| Women | 65-lb barbell |

## Profile
Army Sgt. Keith Adam Coe, 30, of Auburndale, Florida, served with the 1st Battalion, 37th Field Artillery Regiment, 3rd Stryker Brigade Combat Team, 2nd Infantry Division, at Joint Base Lewis-McChord, Washington. He died on April 27, 2010, in Khalis, Iraq, of wounds sustained when enemy forces attacked his unit with an explosive device. He is survived by his wife, Katrina; his sons, Killian and Keith Jr.; and his daughter, Klover. Coe was first posted on crossfit.com on August 12, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Barbell and unstable-surface pressing
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 20-30 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — 100 thrusters set up every ring push-up to shake

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
