# Bert

A long bodyweight chipper of burpees, push-ups, walking lunges, and squats, run between quarter-mile runs.

## Profile

Marine Cpl. Albert Gettings, 27, of New Castle, Pennsylvania, a team leader with 2nd Battalion, 6th Marine Regiment, was killed Jan. 5, 2009, while conducting counter-sniper operations in Fallujah, Iraq.
