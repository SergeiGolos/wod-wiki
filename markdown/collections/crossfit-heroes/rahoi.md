# Rahoi

## Description
A twelve-minute AMRAP of box jumps, thrusters, and bar-facing burpees.

```time
12:00 AMRAP
  12 Box Jumps 24in
  6 Thrusters 95lb
  6 Bar-Facing Burpees
```

## Scaling

| Level | Box | Thrusters |
|-------|-----|-----------|
| Men | 24 in | 95 lb |
| Women | 20 in | 65 lb |

## Profile
FBI Supervisory Special Agent Gregory J. Rahoi, 38, Hostage Rescue Team, killed December 6, 2006 in a live-fire training accident at Fort A.P. Hill, Virginia.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics and barbell conditioning
- **Time Domain:** Short, 12-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Box jump, thruster, bar-facing burpee
- **Intensity:** Sprint-paced; small rounds encourage near-constant movement

## Collectible Metrics

- **Reps:** Total rounds and reps completed
