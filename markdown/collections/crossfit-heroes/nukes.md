# Nukes

## Description
Three continuous segments — 8, 10, and 12 minutes — each pairing a mile run with max reps of a barbell lift.

```time
8:00
  1mile Run
  ? Deadlifts 315lb
10:00
  1mile Run
  ? Power Cleans 225lb
12:00
  1mile Run
  ? Overhead Squats 135lb
```

## Profile
U.S. Marine Capt. Matthew "Nukes" Manoukian, 29, of Los Altos Hills, California, was assigned to the 1st Marine Special Operations Battalion, based at Camp Pendleton, California. He died on Aug. 10, 2012, in the Sangin District of Afghanistan, after being shot by an Afghan policeman. He is survived by his parents, Socrates Peter and Patricia, and his brothers, Mike and Marty. Nukes was first posted on crossfit.com on April 15, 2014.

## Scaling
| Level | Load |
|-------|-------|
| Men | 315-lb deadlift, 225-lb power clean, 135-lb overhead squat |
| Women | 205-lb deadlift, 155-lb power clean, 95-lb overhead squat |

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Monostructural and weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Long, 30 minutes total across the three segments
- **Primary Movements:** Mile runs, max-rep deadlifts, power cleans, overhead squats
- **Intensity:** Maximal — no rest between segments; each lift begins on tired legs

## Collectible Metrics
- **Reps:** Total max reps across the three lifts
