## Profile
U.S. Marine Sgt. Justin M. Hansen, 26, of Traverse City, Michigan, was assigned to the 2nd Marine Special Operations Battalion, based at Camp Lejeune, North Carolina. He died on July 24, 2012, in Badghis Province, Afghanistan, while conducting combat operations. He is survived by his parents, Vickie Hayes and Richard; his stepmother, Shawna; his stepfather, Steven C. Cornell; his sisters, Adrienne Russell, Morgan Compton, and Veronica Compton; his stepbrothers, Jeremy Borey and Adam Cornell; and his stepsister, Jessica Borey. Justin was first posted on crossfit.com on April 10, 2014.
