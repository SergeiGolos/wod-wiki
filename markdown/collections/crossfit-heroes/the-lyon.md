# The Lyon

## Description

Five interval rounds of squat cleans, shoulder-to-overheads, and burpee chest-to-bar pull-ups, taken one round at a time with rest between.

```time
(5)
  7 Squat Cleans 165lb
  7 Shoulder-to-Overheads 165lb
  7 Burpee Chest-to-Bar Pullups
  2:00 Rest
// use a pull-up bar 6in above standing max reach
```

## Scaling

| Level | Squat Clean | Shoulder-to-Overhead | Pullup Bar |
|-------|-------------|----------------------|------------|
| Men | 165 lb | 165 lb | 6 in above standing reach |
| Women | 115 lb | 115 lb | 6 in above standing reach |

## Profile

Capt. David Lyon, 28, of Sandpoint, Idaho, an Air Force officer with the 21st Logistics Readiness Squadron and 2008 Air Force Academy graduate known for his athleticism and leadership, was killed by an IED in Kabul, Afghanistan, on Dec. 27, 2013.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting and gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Moderate interval work, about 15-20 minutes with rests
- **Primary Movements:** Squat clean, shoulder-to-overhead, burpee chest-to-bar pull-up
- **Intensity:** High, repeatable round pace
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (chest-to-bar pull-ups at height)

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all five rounds
