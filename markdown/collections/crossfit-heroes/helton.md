# Helton

## Description
Honors Air Force 1st Lt. Joseph Helton, Security Forces officer killed by an IED near Baghdad, Iraq, in 2009. Three rounds of an 800-meter run, 30 dumbbell squat cleans, and 30 burpees for time.

```time
(3)
  800m Run
  30 Dumbbell Squat Cleans 50lb
  30 Burpees
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 50-lb dumbbells |
| Women | 35-lb dumbbells |

## Profile
Air Force Security Forces 1st Lt. Joseph D. Helton, 24, of Monroe, Georgia, was assigned to the 6th Security Forces Squadron at MacDill Air Force Base, Tampa, Florida. He was killed on September 8, 2009, while on a mission near Baghdad, Iraq, when an improvised explosive device detonated near his vehicle. He is survived by his mother, Jiffy. Helton was first posted on crossfit.com on September 9, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Running, dumbbell strength, and burpees
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 25-35 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — heavy dumbbell cleans burn the legs the runs need next round

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
