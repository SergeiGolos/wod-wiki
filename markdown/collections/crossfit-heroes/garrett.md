# Garrett

## Description
Honors Marine Capt. Garrett "Tubes" Lawton, killed by an IED in Afghanistan in 2008. Three rounds of 75 squats, 25 ring handstand push-ups, and 25 L pull-ups.

```time
(3)
  75 Squats
  25 Ring Handstand Push Ups
  25 L Pull Ups
```

## Profile
Marine Capt. Garrett T. "Tubes" Lawton, 31, of Charleston, West Virginia, was killed by an IED in Herat Province, Afghanistan, on August 4, 2008. He is survived by his wife, Trisha, and two sons, Ryan and Caden. Garrett was first posted on crossfit.com on February 22, 2010.
