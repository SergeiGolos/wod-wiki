# Jennifer

## Profile

Constable Jennifer Kovach, 26, a four-year veteran of the Guelph Police Service, died March 14, 2013, when her cruiser crashed while she responded to a call for service. A 26-minute AMRAP of pull-ups, kettlebell swings, and box jumps.

```time
26:00 AMRAP
  10 Pullups
  15 Kettlebell Swings 53lb
  20 Box Jumps 24in
```
