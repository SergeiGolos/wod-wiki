# JBo

## Description
A 28-minute AMRAP of overhead squats, a legless rope climb from seated, and bench presses.

```time
28:00 AMRAP
  9 Overhead Squats 115lb
  1 Legless Rope Climb 15ft
  12 Bench Presses 115lb
```

## Profile
U.S. Army Staff Sgt. Jeremie S. "JBo" Border, 28, of Mesquite, Texas, was assigned to the 1st Battalion, 1st Special Forces Group (Airborne), based at Torii Station, Okinawa, Japan. He died on Sept. 1, 2012, in Batur Village, Afghanistan, from wounds suffered when enemy forces attacked his unit with small-arms fire. He is survived by his parents, Mary Border and Robert Harris, and his sisters, DeLaynie Peek, Katie Border, Ashley Harris, and Amanda Pereira. JBo was first posted on crossfit.com on Aug. 16, 2014.
