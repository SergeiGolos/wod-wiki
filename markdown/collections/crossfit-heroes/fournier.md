# Fournier

## Description

A couplet of shoulder-to-overheads and sumo deadlift high pulls separated by heavy arm-over-arm sled pulls.

```time
For time:
50 Shoulder-to-Overheads 115lb
50ft Sled Pull
40 Burpees
50ft Sled Pull
30 Sumo Deadlift High Pulls 85lb
50ft Sled Pull
// sled load: challenging but no extended rest
```

## Scaling

| Level | Shoulder-to-Overhead | Sumo Deadlift High Pull | Sled Pull |
|-------|----------------------|-------------------------|-----------|
| Men | 115 lb | 85 lb | Challenging load |
| Women | 75 lb | 55 lb | Challenging load |

## Profile

Mike Fournier, 52, a CrossFit Level 2 Trainer who coached at Cyn Gym CrossFit and flew firefighting missions under contract for Cal Fire, died Aug. 19, 2020, when his helicopter crashed while fighting the Hills Fire near Coalinga, California.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting and strongman
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, roughly 10-20 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Shoulder-to-overhead, sled pull, burpee, sumo deadlift high pull
- **Intensity:** High
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
