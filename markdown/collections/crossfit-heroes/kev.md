# Kev

## Description
Honors Dutch commando Cpl. Kevin van de Rijdt. With a partner, a 26-minute AMRAP of 6 deadlifts each, 9 synchronized bar-facing burpees, 9 bar muscle-ups each, and a 55-foot partner barbell carry.

```time
26:00 AMRAP
  6 Deadlifts 315lb
  9 Synchronized Bar-facing Burpees
  9 Bar Muscle-ups
  55ft Partner Barbell Carry 315lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 315-lb barbell |
| Women | 205-lb barbell |

## Profile
Operator Cpl. Kevin van de Rijdt, 26, of the Netherlands, died Sept. 6, 2009, during heavy combat in Afghanistan. He was a member of Special Forces (Korps Commando Troepen) Task Force 55 within the Netherlands Armed Forces. First posted on crossfit.com on September 6, 2017.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Weightlifting and gymnastics, partner-paced
- **Time Domain:** Fixed, 26-minute partner AMRAP
- **Primary Movements:** Deadlifts, synchronized bar-facing burpees, bar muscle-ups, partner barbell carry
- **Intensity:** Very high — synchronization forces shared pacing
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (bar muscle-ups and heavy carries)

## Collectible Metrics
- **Reps:** Total team rounds and reps completed
