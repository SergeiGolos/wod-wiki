# Thompson

## Description
Honors Army Capt. David Thompson, Special Forces commander killed in Wardak Province, Afghanistan, in 2010. Ten rounds of a rope climb begun from seated, 29 back squats, and a 10-meter barbell farmers carry for time.

```time
(10)
  1 Rope Climb 15ft
  29 Back Squats 95lb
  10m Farmers Carry 135lb
```

| Sex | Loads |

| Women | 65-lb back squat, 95-lb farmers carry |

## Profile
Army Capt. David J. Thompson, 39, of Hooker, Oklahoma, commanded Operational Detachment Alpha 3334, Company C, 3rd Battalion, 3rd Special Forces Group (Airborne), based at Fort Bragg, North Carolina. He was killed on January 29, 2010, while supporting combat operations in Wardak Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his wife, Emily; his daughters, Isabelle and Abigail; his parents, Charles and Freida; and his sister, Alisha Mueller. Thompson was first posted on crossfit.com on November 30, 2010.

- **Modality:** Climbing, squatting volume, and carrying
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 35-50 minutes
- **Intensity:** Relentless — 290 back squats punish the legs long after the climbs end
