# T.J.

## Description
Honors Marine Pfc. Anthony "TJ" Antell Jr. Repeat a triplet of 10 bench presses, 10 strict pull-ups, and a max set of thrusters until 100 thrusters are complete.

```time
(?)
  10 Bench Press 185lb
  10 Strict Pullups
  ? Thrusters 135lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 185-lb bench press, 135-lb thruster |
| Women | 125-lb bench press, 95-lb thruster |

## Profile
United States Marine Corps Pfc. Anthony "TJ" Antell Jr., 35, of Arlington, Texas, died May 2, 2016, from a gunshot wound suffered when he tried to disarm a gunman at a local Walgreens. Antell was the owner of CrossFit Abattoir. First posted on crossfit.com on July 3, 2016.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Weightlifting and strict gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Moderate — the thruster count is capped at 100
- **Primary Movements:** Bench press, strict pull-ups, thrusters
- **Intensity:** High — max thruster sets shrink each cycle
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate (strict pull-up capacity)

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to reach 100 thrusters
