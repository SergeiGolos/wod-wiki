# Arnie

## Description
Honors Los Angeles County Firefighter Specialist Arnaldo "Arnie" Quinones, killed in the 2009 Station Fire. A single-kettlebell chipper of Turkish get-ups, swings, and overhead squats, alternating arms.

```time
21 Turkish Get Ups 70lb Right Arm
50 KB Swings 70lb
21 Overhead Squats 70lb Left Arm
50 KB Swings 70lb
21 Overhead Squats 70lb Right Arm
50 KB Swings 70lb
21 Turkish Get Ups 70lb Left Arm
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |

| Men | 70-lb kettlebell |

## Profile
Los Angeles County Firefighter Specialist Arnaldo "Arnie" Quinones, 34, was killed in the line of duty on August 30, 2009, during the Station Fire, when his emergency-response vehicle went over the road edge and fell 800 feet into a canyon during fire suppression protecting Camp 16 outside Palmdale, California. He is survived by his wife, Lori, and daughter, Sophia Grace, born three weeks after his death. Arnie was first posted on crossfit.com on May 29, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Single-kettlebell strength and stability
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 30-45 minutes
- **Intensity:** Grinding — 42 get-ups and 150 swings demand steady pacing over sprinting
