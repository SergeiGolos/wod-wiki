# Larry

## Description

A descending couplet of front squats and bar-facing burpees from 21 down to 3.

```time
(21-18-15-12-9-6-3) Front Squats 115lb / Bar-Facing Burpees
```

## Scaling

| Level | Front Squat |
|-------|-------------|
| Men | 115 lb |
| Women | 75 lb |

## Profile

Marine Cpl. Larry D. Harris Jr., 25, a fire team leader with 3rd Squad, 81mm Mortar Platoon, Weapons Company, 3rd Battalion 1st Marines, was killed in a firefight in Helmand Province, Afghanistan, on July 1, 2010, and posthumously awarded the Silver Star.
