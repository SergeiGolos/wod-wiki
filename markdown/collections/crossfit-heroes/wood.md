# Wood

## Description
Five rounds of 400-meter runs, burpee box jumps, sumo deadlift high pulls, and thrusters, resting one minute between rounds.

```time
(5)
  Run 400m
  10 Burpee Box Jumps 24in
  10 Sumo Deadlift High Pulls 95lb
  10 Thrusters 95lb
  1:00 Rest
```

## Scaling

| Level | Box | Barbell |
|-------|-----|---------|
| Men | 24 in | 95 lb |
| Women | 20 in | 65 lb |

## Profile
Australian Army Sgt. Brett Wood, MG recipient, 32, 2nd Commando Regiment, killed May 23, 2011 in Helmand Province, Afghanistan, by an insurgent IED attack.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Mixed monostructural and barbell
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, about 25 minutes with rest periods
- **Primary Movements:** Run, burpee box jump, sumo deadlift high pull, thruster
- **Intensity:** High, repeated sprint-effort rounds

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all five rounds
