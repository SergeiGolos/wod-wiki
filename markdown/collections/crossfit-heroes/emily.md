# Emily

## Description
Honors Army 2nd Lt. Emily Jazmin Tatum Perez. Ten rounds, each for time, of 30 double-unders, 15 pull-ups, 30 squats, and a 100-meter sprint, resting 2 minutes between rounds.

```time
(10)
  + 30 Double-unders
  + 15 Pullups
  + 30 Squats
  + 100m Sprint
  2:00 Rest
```

## Profile
Second Lt. Emily Jazmin Tatum Perez, 23, was killed Sept. 12, 2006, when her Humvee was struck by an improvised explosive device as she led a convoy through Al Kifl, Iraq. She served in the 204th Support Battalion, 2nd Brigade, 4th Infantry Division, and graduated from the U.S. Military Academy in 2005. First posted on crossfit.com on September 10, 2017.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics and monostructural sprints
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, repeated intervals with 2-minute rests
- **Primary Movements:** Double-unders, pull-ups, squats, 100-meter sprints
- **Intensity:** High — each round is an all-out effort

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Time of each round
