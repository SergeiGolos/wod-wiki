# Feeks

## Description

## Profile
U.S. Navy Special Warfare Operator Petty Officer 1st Class Patrick D. Feeks, 28, of Edgewater, Maryland, was assigned to a Naval Special Warfare unit based on the West Coast. He died on Aug. 16, 2012, in a helicopter crash northeast of Kandahar, Afghanistan, while supporting Operation Enduring Freedom. He is survived by his parents, Thomas and Ginny; his sister, Regina; and his wife, Emily. Feeks was first posted on crossfit.com on Oct. 3, 2014.
