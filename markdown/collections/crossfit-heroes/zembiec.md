# Zembiec

## Description
Five rounds of back squats, strict burpee pull-ups, and a 400-meter run.

```time
(5)
  11 Back Squats 185lb
  7 Strict Burpee Pull Ups
  400m Run
```

## Profile
U.S. Marine Maj. Douglas A. Zembiec, 34, of Albuquerque, New Mexico, was assigned to Headquarters Battalion, Marine Corps National Capital Region, Henderson Hall, based in Arlington, Virginia. He was killed during a firefight on May 11, 2007, in Baghdad, Iraq. He is survived by his wife, Pamela; his daughter, Fallyn; his parents, Donald and Jo Ann; and his brother, John. Zembiec was first posted on crossfit.com on May 9, 2014.
