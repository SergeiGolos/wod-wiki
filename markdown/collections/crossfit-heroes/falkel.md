# Falkel

## Description
A 25-minute AMRAP of handstand push-ups, box jumps, and a 15-foot rope climb in steady eights.

```time
25:00 AMRAP
  8 Handstand Push Ups
  8 Box Jumps 30in
  1 Rope Climb 15ft
```

## Profile
U.S. Army Staff Sgt. Chris Falkel, 22, of Highlands Ranch, Colorado, was assigned to the 1st Battalion, 3rd Special Forces Group, based at Fort Bragg, North Carolina. He was killed by enemy small-arms fire on Aug. 8, 2005, in Deh Afghan, Afghanistan. He is survived by his parents, Jeff and Dianne. Falkel was first posted on crossfit.com on Aug. 20, 2013.
