# Paul Pena

## Description
Honors Army Capt. Paul W. Pena. Seven rounds, each for time, of a 100-meter sprint, 19 kettlebell swings, and 10 burpee box jumps, resting 3 minutes between rounds.

```time
(7)
  + 100m Sprint
  + 19 KB Swings 70lb
  + 10 Burpee Box Jumps 24in
  3:00 Rest
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 70-lb kettlebell, 24-inch box |
| Women | 53-lb kettlebell, 20-inch box |

## Profile
United States Army Capt. Paul W. Pena, 27, a 2004 West Point graduate from San Marcos, Texas, died Jan. 19, 2010, of wounds from an explosive in the Arghandab River Valley of Afghanistan. He was assigned to Fort Bragg, North Carolina. First posted on crossfit.com on January 19, 2017.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Monostructural sprint and kettlebell power
- **Time Domain:** Short, repeated intervals with 3-minute rests
- **Primary Movements:** 100-meter sprints, kettlebell swings, burpee box jumps
- **Intensity:** High — each round is a sprint

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Time of each round
