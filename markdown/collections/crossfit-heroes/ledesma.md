# Ledesma

## Description
Honors Chandler (Arizona) Police Detective Carlos Ledesma, killed in an undercover narcotics operation in 2010. A 20-minute AMRAP of parallette handstand push-ups, toes-to-rings, and medicine-ball cleans.

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  5 Parallette Handstand Push Ups
  10 Toes To Rings
  15 Medicine Ball Cleans 20lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 20-lb medicine ball |
| Women | 14-lb medicine ball |

## Profile
Chandler Police Department Narcotics Detective and Special Assignment Unit operator Carlos Ledesma, 34, was shot and killed by drug dealers on July 28, 2010, during an undercover operation in Phoenix, Arizona. He is survived by his wife, Sherry, and his sons, Luciano and Elijo. Ledesma was first posted on crossfit.com on February 8, 2011.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics and odd-object conditioning
- **Time Domain:** Fixed, 20-minute AMRAP
- **Intensity:** High — handstand work taxes the shoulders before every clean

## Collectible Metrics
- **Reps:** Total rounds and reps completed
