# McGhee

## Description
Honors Army Cpl. Ryan C. McGhee of the 75th Ranger Regiment, killed in Iraq in 2009. A 30-minute AMRAP of 5 deadlifts at 275 lb, 13 push-ups, and 9 box jumps.

```time
30:00 AMRAP
  5 Deadlifts 275lb
  13 Push Ups
  9 Box Jumps 24in
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 275-lb barbell, 24-inch box |
| Women | 185-lb barbell, 20-inch box |

## Profile
Army Cpl. Ryan C. McGhee, 21, served with 3rd Battalion, 75th Ranger Regiment at Fort Benning, Georgia. He was killed in action by small-arms fire during combat in central Iraq on May 13, 2009 — his fourth deployment and first to Iraq. He is survived by his father, Steven; mother, Sherrie; fiancee, Ashleigh; and brother, Zachary. McGhee was first posted on crossfit.com on April 15, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Heavy barbell, push-ups, box jumps
- **Time Domain:** Fixed, 30-minute AMRAP
- **Intensity:** High — heavy deadlifts every round force the legs to run the clock

## Collectible Metrics
- **Reps:** Total rounds and reps completed
