# TPT9000

## Description

A chipper of 100-meter runs and 9 rounds of 8 burpees, 26 kettlebell swings, and 21 wall-ball shots, followed by the companion hero workouts that share its official collection chapter, each listed below with its own whiteboard.

```time
For time:
100m Run
(9)
  8 Burpees
  26 KB Swings 44lb
  21 Wall Ball Shots 20lb to 10ft
100m Run
// if you have a 14/20lb weight vest, wear it
```

## Scaling

| Level | Kettlebell | Wall Ball |
|-------|------------|-----------|
| Men | 44 lb | 20 lb to 10 ft |
| Women | 35 lb | 14 lb to 9 ft |

## Profile

Army Staff Sgt. Ryan Christian Knauss, 23, of Corryton, Tennessee, a psychological operations soldier with the 9th Battalion, 8th Psychological Operations Group (Airborne), died Aug. 26, 2021, of wounds from the suicide attack at Hamid Karzai International Airport in Kabul, where he supported the noncombatant evacuation. The rep scheme (9-8-26-21) encodes his unit and the date of the attack; the name and event honor all those lost at the Abbey Gate.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Metabolic conditioning
- **Time Domain:** Main workout moderate, 15-25 minutes; the companion sections below are standalone hero workouts of varying length
- **Primary Movements:** Run, burpee, kettlebell swing, wall-ball shot
- **Intensity:** Sustained high
- **Skill Level:** Beginner
