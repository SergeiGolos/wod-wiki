# Jerry

## Description
Honors Army Sgt. Maj. Jerry Patton, killed during HAHO training in 2008. A mile run, 2K row, and another mile run — pure monostructural endurance for time.

```time
1 Mile Run
2000m Row
1 Mile Run
```

## Profile
Army Sgt. Maj. Jerry Dwayne Patton, 40, served with the U.S. Army USSOCOM and was preparing for deployment to Afghanistan. He died on October 15, 2008, during High Altitude High Opening (HAHO) training. He is survived by his wife, Molly, and sons, Chad, Cody, Chase, and Connor. Jerry was first posted on crossfit.com on May 9, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Monostructural endurance
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 25-35 minutes
- **Intensity:** Sustained aerobic — pacing the transitions decides the time

## Collectible Metrics
