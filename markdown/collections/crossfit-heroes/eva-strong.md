# Eva Strong

## Description

A five-round partner workout pairing double-unders and toes-to-bars with clean and jerks and shared runs.

```time
With a partner:
(5)
  24 Double Unders each
  19 Toes-to-Bars total
  2 Clean and Jerks 205lb total
  400m Run together
```

## Scaling

| Level | Clean and Jerk |
|-------|----------------|
| Men | 205 lb |
| Women | 135 lb |

## Profile

Eva Mireles, 44, a fourth-grade teacher in Uvalde, Texas, and nine-time CrossFit Open participant, died May 24, 2022, while shielding her students from a gunman at Robb Elementary School.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Gymnastics, weightlifting, and metabolic conditioning
- **Time Domain:** Moderate-long, roughly 20-30 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Double-under, toes-to-bar, clean and jerk, run
- **Intensity:** High, partner-paced
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all five rounds
