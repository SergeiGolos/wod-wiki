# Adam Brown

## Description
Honors Navy SEAL Chief Special Warfare Operator Adam Lee Brown, killed in combat in Komar Province, Afghanistan, in 2010. Two rounds of 24s — deadlifts, box jumps, wall-ball shots, bench presses, more jumps and balls, then cleans — for time.

```time
(2)
  24 Deadlifts 295lb
  24 Box Jumps 24in
  24 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
  24 Bench Presses 195lb
  24 Box Jumps 24in
  24 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
  24 Cleans 145lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 295-lb deadlifts, 24-inch box, 20-lb ball to 10 feet, 195-lb bench press, 145-lb cleans |
| Women | 95-lb deadlifts, 20-inch box, 14-lb ball to 9 feet, 135-lb bench press, 95-lb cleans |

## Profile
Navy Chief Special Warfare Operator (SEAL) Adam Lee Brown, 36, of Hot Springs, Arkansas, was killed on March 17, 2010, in Komar Province, Afghanistan, in a battle against heavily armed militants. He is survived by his wife, Kelley; his two children, Nathan and Savannah; and his parents. Adam Brown was first posted on crossfit.com on August 10, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Heavy barbell and volume conditioning
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 30-45 minutes
- **Intensity:** Brutal — 168 total reps with three heavy bars waiting inside them

## Collectible Metrics
