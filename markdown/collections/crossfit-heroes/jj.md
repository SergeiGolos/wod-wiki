# J.J.

## Description
A ladder pairing squat cleans with parallette handstand push-ups, climbing from one to ten reps while the push-ups descend from ten to one.

## Profile
U.S. Marine Corps Lance Cpl. Justin James "JJ" Wilson, 24, 3rd Battalion, 10th Marine Regiment, killed March 22, 2010 supporting combat operations in Helmand Province, Afghanistan.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Barbell and gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 15-25 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Squat clean, parallette handstand push-up
- **Intensity:** High; clean volume doubles as push-up capacity fades
