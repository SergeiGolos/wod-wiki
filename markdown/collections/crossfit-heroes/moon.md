# Moon

## Description
Seven rounds pairing single-arm dumbbell hang split snatches with rope climbs, alternating arms each round.

```time
(7)
  10 Right-Arm Dumbbell Hang Split Snatches 40lb
  1 Rope Climb 15ft
  10 Left-Arm Dumbbell Hang Split Snatches 40lb
  1 Rope Climb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Dumbbell |
|-------|----------|
| Men | 40 lb |
| Women | 30 lb |

## Profile
U.S. Army Spc. Christopher Moon, 20, 2nd Battalion, 508th Parachute Infantry Regiment, 82nd Airborne Division, died July 13, 2010 from injuries in an IED attack in Arghandab, Afghanistan.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Dumbbell work and rope gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Long, 25-40 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Dumbbell hang split snatch (alternate feet each rep), rope climb
- **Intensity:** Grindy; grip endurance is the limiting factor

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all seven rounds
