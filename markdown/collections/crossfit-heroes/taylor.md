# Taylor

## Description
Four rounds of a 400-meter run and five burpee muscle-ups; wear a weighted vest or body armor if you have one.

```time
(4)
  400m Run
  5 Burpee Muscle Ups
```

## Profile
U.S. Army Specialist David Wayne Taylor, 20, of Dixon, Kentucky, was assigned to the 2nd Battalion, 508th Parachute Infantry Regiment, 4th Brigade Combat Team, 82nd Airborne Division, based at Fort Bragg, North Carolina. He died on March 29, 2012, in Kandahar Province, Afghanistan, of wounds sustained in an enemy attack. He is survived by his sisters, Tamara Taylor and Christina Abell, and his mother, Sarah Whitledge Taylor. Taylor was first posted on crossfit.com on April 6, 2014.
