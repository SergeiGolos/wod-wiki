# Kerrie

## Description

Ten sprint-led rounds of burpees, sit-ups, and push-ups in a weight vest, resting two minutes each round.

```time
(10)
  100m Sprint
  5 Burpees
  20 Situps
  15 Pushups
  100m Sprint
  2:00 Rest
// wear a weight vest
```

## Scaling

| Level | Vest |
|-------|------|
| Men | Weight vest |
| Women | Weight vest |

## Profile

Omaha Police Officer Kerrie Orozco, 29, a seven-year veteran and community volunteer with the Special Olympics, was killed in the line of duty on May 20, 2015, just hours before starting maternity leave.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Metabolic conditioning and gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Moderate interval work, about 20-30 minutes with rests
- **Primary Movements:** Sprint, burpee, sit-up, push-up
- **Intensity:** Very high per round
- **Skill Level:** Beginner

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all ten rounds
