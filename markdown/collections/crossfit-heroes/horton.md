# Horton

## Profile

Army Spc. Christopher D. Horton, 26, of the Oklahoma National Guard's 45th Infantry Brigade Combat Team, died September 9, 2011, in Zurmat District, Afghanistan, from small-arms fire during an enemy attack. A partner workout: nine rounds of bar muscle-ups, clean and jerks, and 50-yard buddy carries; solo, do 5 reps per round and carry a heavy sandbag.

```time
(9) with a partner
  9 Bar Muscle-Ups
  11 Clean and Jerks 155lb
  50yd Buddy Carry
```

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Gymnastics, weightlifting, and loaded carry
- **Time Domain:** Long, no cap
- **Primary Movements:** Bar muscle-up, clean and jerk, buddy carry
- **Intensity:** High; one partner works at a time
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (bar muscle-ups)
