# Big Sexy

## Profile

Sgt. Lance "Big Sexy" McLean, 38, a Hood County Sheriff's Office SWAT member in Texas, died June 29, 2013, from a gunshot wound sustained in the line of duty the day before. Five rounds descending 6-5-4: deadlifts and burpees, cleans and chest-to-bar pull-ups, thrusters and muscle-ups.

```time
(5)
  6 Deadlifts 315lb
  6 Burpees
  5 Cleans 225lb
  5 Chest-to-Bar Pullups
  4 Thrusters 155lb
  4 Muscle-Ups
```

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting, gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Long, no cap

- **Intensity:** Very high; heavy barbell work plus gymnastics volume
- **Skill Level:** Elite (muscle-ups at 20 total reps)
