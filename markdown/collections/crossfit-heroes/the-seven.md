# The Seven

## Description
Honors the seven CIA officers killed in the December 2009 suicide bombing at a remote base in southeastern Afghanistan. Seven rounds of seven reps across seven movements for time.

```time
(7)
  7 Handstand Push Ups
  7 Thrusters 135lb
  7 Knees To Elbows
  7 Deadlifts 245lb
  7 Burpees
  7 KB Swings 70lb
  7 Pull Ups
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 135-lb thrusters, 245-lb deadlifts, 70-lb kettlebell |
| Women | 95-lb thrusters, 165-lb deadlifts, 53-lb kettlebell |

## Profile
A suicide bomber posing as a potential informant killed seven CIA officers at a remote base in southeastern Afghanistan on December 30, 2009: Jennifer Lynne Matthews, 45; Scott Michael Roberson, 39; Harold E. Brown Jr., 37; Darren LaBonte, 35; Elizabeth Hanson, 30; and security contractors Jeremy Jason Wise, 35, and Dane Clark Paresi, 46. The Seven was first posted on crossfit.com on May 30, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics, barbell, and engine — the full triplet spectrum
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 30-45 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — sevens keep movements fresh but the rounds never stop coming

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
