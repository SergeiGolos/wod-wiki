# Hammer

## Description
Honors Army 1st Sgt. Michael "Hammer" Bordelon, who died of wounds from a car bomb in Mosul, Iraq, in 2005. Five rounds, each for time, of power cleans, front squats, jerks, and pull-ups with 90 seconds of rest between rounds.

```time
(5)
  5 Power Cleans 135lb
  10 Front Squats 135lb
  5 Jerks 135lb
  20 Pull Ups
  1:30 Rest
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 135-lb barbell |
| Women | 95-lb barbell |

## Profile
Army 1st Sgt. Michael "Hammer" Bordelon, 37, of Morgan City, Louisiana, was assigned to the 1st Battalion, 24th Infantry Regiment, 1st Brigade, 25th Infantry Division (Stryker Brigade Combat Team), based at Fort Lewis, Washington. He was wounded when a car bomb exploded near him in Mosul, Iraq, on April 23, 2005, and died of those injuries on May 10, 2005. He is survived by his wife, Mila; his children, Mike Jr., Jacob, and Johanna; his mother, Dolores; and his sister, Doreen Scioneaux. Hammer was first posted on crossfit.com on April 17, 2011.
