# Luce

## Description
Honors Army Capt. Ronald G. Luce, Special Forces soldier killed by a command-wire IED in Qole Gerdsar, Afghanistan, in 2009. Three rounds, wearing a weight vest, of a 1,000-meter run, muscle-ups, and 100 squats for time.

```time
(3)
  1000m Run
  10 Muscle Ups
  100 Air Squats
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 20-lb weight vest |
| Women | 14-lb weight vest |

## Profile
Army Capt. Ronald G. Luce, 27, of Company C, 2nd Battalion, 20th Special Forces Group, headquartered in Jackson, Mississippi, died on August 2, 2009, in Qole Gerdsar, Afghanistan, when his vehicle was struck by a command-wire improvised explosive device. He is survived by his wife, Kendahl Shoemaker; his 5-year-old daughter, Carrie; and his parents, Ronald and Katherine. Luce was first posted on crossfit.com on July 5, 2010.

- **Modality:** Running and gymnastics under load
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 25-35 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — the vest turns 300 squats into a leg-burning grind
