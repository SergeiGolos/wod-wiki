# Ricky

## Description
A twenty-minute AMRAP of pull-ups, dumbbell deadlifts, and push presses.

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  10 Pullups
  5 Dumbbell Deadlifts 75lb
  8 Push Presses 75lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Dumbbells | Barbell |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Men | 75 lb | 135 lb |
| Women | 50 lb | 95 lb |

## Profile
U.S. Army Sgt. William "Ricky" Rudd, 27, 3rd Battalion, 75th Ranger Regiment, died October 5, 2008 from enemy small-arms fire on a combat patrol in Mosul, Iraq.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics and dumbbell strength endurance
- **Time Domain:** Short, 20-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Pull-up, dumbbell deadlift, push press
- **Intensity:** High, small fast rounds

## Collectible Metrics

- **Reps:** Total rounds and reps completed
