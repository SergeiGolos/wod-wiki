# Artie

## Description
Honors police officer Arthur "Artie" Lopez. A 20-minute AMRAP of 5 pull-ups, 10 push-ups, 15 squats, 5 more pull-ups, and 10 thrusters.

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  5 Pullups
  10 Pushups
  15 Squats
  5 Pullups
  10 Thrusters 95lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 95-lb thruster |
| Women | 65-lb thruster |

## Profile
Police Officer Arthur "Artie" Lopez, 29, of Babylon Village, New York, was killed in the line of duty on Oct. 23, 2012. A decorated eight-year veteran, he was serving on the Emergency Services Unit of the Nassau County Police Department at the time of his death. First posted on crossfit.com on October 23, 2016.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics and weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Fixed, 20-minute AMRAP
- **Primary Movements:** Pull-ups, push-ups, squats, thrusters
- **Intensity:** High — thrusters cap each round
