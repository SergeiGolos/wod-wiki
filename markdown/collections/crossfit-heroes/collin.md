# Collin

## Description
Honors Navy SEAL Chief Special Warfare Operator Collin Thomas, fatally shot during combat operations in eastern Afghanistan in 2010. Six rounds of a 400-meter sandbag carry, push presses, box jumps, and sumo deadlift high pulls for time.

```time
(6)
  400m Sandbag Carry 50lb
  12 Push Presses 115lb
  12 Box Jumps 24in
  12 Sumo Deadlift High Pulls 95lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 50-lb sandbag, 115-lb push press, 24-inch box, 95-lb sumo deadlift high pull |
| Women | 35-lb sandbag, 75-lb push press, 20-inch box, 65-lb sumo deadlift high pull |

## Profile
Navy Special Warfare Operator Chief (SEAL) Collin Trent Thomas, 33, of Morehead, Kentucky, was assigned to a Navy SEAL team based at Little Creek, Virginia. He was fatally shot on August 18, 2010, during combat operations in eastern Afghanistan. He is survived by his fiancée, Sarah Saunders; his parents, Clay and Jean Thomas; and his sister, Meghan Edwards. Collin was first posted on crossfit.com on November 21, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Odd-object carry, barbell, and jumping
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 30-40 minutes
- **Intensity:** Grueling — six heavy carries before grip-heavy high pulls each round

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
