# Dragon

## Description
Two 5K runs bracketing four-minute windows to find a 4-rep-max deadlift and push jerk.

```time
5km Run
4:00 4 Deadlift ?lb
5km Run
4:00 4 Push Jerk ?lb
```

## Profile
U.S. Army Capt. Nicholas S. Rozanski, 36, of Dublin, Ohio, was assigned to the 1st Battalion, 148th Infantry Regiment, 37th Infantry Brigade Combat Team, Ohio National Guard, based in Walbridge, Ohio. He died on April 4, 2012, of wounds sustained during an enemy attack in Faryab Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his wife, Jennifer; his daughters, Emma and Anna; his mother, Pamela Mitchell; his father, Jan; and his brothers, Keith and Alex. Dragon was first posted on crossfit.com on Oct. 5, 2013.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Monostructural and weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Long, roughly 30-40 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** 5K runs, 4-rep-max deadlift, 4-rep-max push jerk
- **Intensity:** Unique — sprint a 5K, then produce maximal strength on a cold press

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
- **Weight:** 4-rep-max deadlift and push jerk
