# Rankel

## Description
Honors Marine Sgt. John Rankel, killed in Helmand Province, Afghanistan, in 2010. A 20-minute AMRAP of deadlifts, burpee pull-ups, kettlebell swings, and a 200-meter run.

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  6 Deadlifts 225lb
  7 Burpee Pull Ups
  10 KB Swings 70lb
  200m Run
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 225-lb barbell, 70-lb kettlebell |
| Women | 155-lb barbell, 53-lb kettlebell |

## Profile
Marine Sgt. John Rankel, 23, of Speedway, Indiana, was assigned to the 3rd Battalion, 1st Marine Regiment, 1st Marine Division, I Marine Expeditionary Force, based at Camp Pendleton, California. He was killed on June 7, 2010, while supporting combat operations in Helmand Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his mother and stepfather, Don and Trisha Stockhoff; his father and stepmother, Kevin and Kim; and his brothers, Nathan Stockhoff and Tyler. Rankel was first posted on crossfit.com on January 7, 2011.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Barbell, gymnastics, kettlebell, and short running
- **Time Domain:** Fixed, 20-minute AMRAP
- **Intensity:** High — burpee pull-ups shred the grip right before each swing set
