# Liam

## Description
Honors Army Sgt. 1st Class Liam J. Nevins. Plate-carry runs bracket 100 toes-to-bars, 50 front squats, and 10 rope climbs, partitioned as needed.

```time
800m Run 45lb
100 Toes-to-bars
50 Front Squats 155lb
10 Rope Climbs
800m Run 45lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 45-lb plate, 155-lb barbell |
| Women | 25-lb plate, 105-lb barbell |

## Profile
U.S. Army Sgt. 1st Class Liam J. Nevins, 32, of Middlebury, Vermont, was killed by small arms fire while conducting combat operations in Paktia Province, Afghanistan, on Sept. 21, 2013. First posted on crossfit.com on March 21, 2017.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Monostructural, gymnastics, and weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Long — high-rep middle with weighted runs
- **Primary Movements:** Plate runs, toes-to-bars, front squats, 15-foot rope climbs
- **Intensity:** Very high — grip and hip fatigue stack
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (rope climbs and 100 toes-to-bars)

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
