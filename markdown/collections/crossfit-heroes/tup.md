# T.U.P.

## Profile

Army Staff Sgt. Michael H. Simpson, 30, "The Unquiet Professional," of the 1st Special Forces Group, died May 1, 2013, of wounds from an April 27 improvised-explosive-device strike in Afghanistan. A 15-12-9-6-3 pyramid of power cleans and front squats with pull-ups repeated each round; his family's nonprofit honors Gold Star Families.

```time
(15-12-9-6-3)
  Power Cleans 135lb
  Pullups
  Front Squats 135lb
  Pullups
```
