# Kelly Brown

## Description

Five rounds of rowing, box jumps, deadlifts, and wall-ball shots.

```time
(5)
  440m Row
  10 Box Jumps 30in
  10 Deadlifts 275lb
  10 Wall Ball Shots 30lb to 10ft
```

## Scaling

| Level | Wall Ball | Deadlift | Box |
|-------|-----------|----------|-----|
| Men | 30 lb to 10 ft | 275 lb | 30 in |
| Women | 20 lb to 9 ft | 185 lb | 24 in |

## Profile

Kelly Brown, 44, a CrossFit Kids Seminar Staff Flowmaster who co-founded CrossFit Agoge in 2007, died Sept. 15, 2020, after a five-year battle with ovarian cancer.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Metabolic conditioning and weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, roughly 15-25 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Row, box jump, deadlift, wall-ball shot
- **Intensity:** High, sustainable round pace
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all five rounds
