# Pheezy

## Description
Three rounds of front squats, pull-ups, deadlifts, toes-to-bar, push jerks, and hand-release push-ups.

```time
(3)
  5 Front Squats 165lb
  18 Pullups
  5 Deadlifts 225lb
  18 Toes to Bar
  5 Push Jerks 165lb
  18 Hand-Release Pushups
```

## Scaling

| Level | Front Squats | Deadlifts | Push Jerks |
|-------|--------------|-----------|------------|
| Men | 165 lb | 225 lb | 165 lb |
| Women | 115 lb | 155 lb | 115 lb |

## Profile
U.S. Marine Corps Lance Cpl. Philip P. Clark, 19, 1st Battalion, 6th Marine Regiment, died May 18, 2010 supporting combat operations in Helmand Province, Afghanistan.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Heavy barbell and gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 15-25 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Front squat, pull-up, deadlift, toes-to-bar, push jerk, hand-release push-up
- **Intensity:** Heavy-then-fast structure; low barbell reps demand unbroken strength

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all three rounds
