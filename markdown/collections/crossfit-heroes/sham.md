# Sham

## Description
Seven rounds of 11 bodyweight deadlifts and a 100-meter sprint.

```time
(7)
  11 Deadlifts 1bw
  100m Sprint
```

## Profile
U.S. Air Force Staff Sgt. David C. "Sham" Wieger, 28, of North Huntingdon, Pennsylvania, was a special agent for the Air Force Office of Special Investigations, Detachment 303, Travis Air Force Base, California. He died on Nov. 1, 2007, near Balad Air Base, Iraq, of wounds sustained from an improvised explosive device that struck his vehicle. He is survived by his parents, Michael and Loreene, and his brother, Michael. Sham was first posted on crossfit.com on Oct. 31, 2014.
