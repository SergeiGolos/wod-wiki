# Bulger

## Description
Honors Canadian Cpl. Nicholas Bulger, killed by an IED on patrol in Zhari District, Afghanistan, in 2009. Ten rounds of a 150-meter run, chest-to-bar pull-ups, front squats, and handstand push-ups for time.

```time
(10)
  150m Run
  7 Chest to Bar Pullups
  7 Front Squats 135lb
  7 Handstand Push Ups
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 135-lb barbell |
| Women | 95-lb barbell |

## Profile
Canadian Forces Cpl. Nicholas Bulger, 30, of Peterborough, Ontario, served with the 3rd Battalion, Princess Patricia's Canadian Light Infantry, based in Edmonton, Alberta. He died on July 3, 2009, while on patrol in the Zhari District of Afghanistan when an improvised explosive device detonated near his vehicle. He is survived by his wife, Rebeka, and daughters Brookelynn and Elizabeth. Bulger was first posted on crossfit.com on October 19, 2010.
