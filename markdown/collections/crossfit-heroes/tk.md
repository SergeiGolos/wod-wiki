# TK

## Description
A 20-minute AMRAP of strict pull-ups, box jumps, and kettlebell swings.

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  8 Strict Pull Ups
  8 Box Jumps 36in
  12 Kettlebell Swings 70lb
```

## Profile
U.S. Army Maj. Thomas E. Kennedy, 35, of West Point, New York, was assigned to Headquarters and Headquarters Company, 4th Brigade Combat Team, 4th Infantry Division, based at Fort Carson, Colorado. He died on Aug. 8, 2012, of wounds suffered when an insurgent detonated a suicide vest in Kunar Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his wife, Kami; his son, Brody; his daughter, Margaret; his parents, George and Patricia; and his brothers, John and George. TK was first posted on crossfit.com on Dec. 28, 2013.
