# Wittman

## Description
Honors Army Sgt. Jeremiah Wittman, killed by a roadside bomb in Afghanistan in 2010. Seven rounds of 15 kettlebell swings, 15 power cleans, and 15 box jumps for time.

```time
(7)
  15 KB Swings 53lb
  15 Power Cleans 95lb
  15 Box Jumps 24in
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 53-lb kettlebell, 95-lb barbell, 24-inch box |
| Women | 35-lb kettlebell, 65-lb barbell, 20-inch box |

## Profile
Army Sgt. Jeremiah Wittman, 26, of Darby, Montana, was assigned to the 1st Battalion, 12th Infantry Regiment, 4th Brigade Combat Team, 4th Infantry Division, based at Fort Carson, Colorado. He was killed on February 13, 2010, when insurgents attacked his unit with a roadside bomb in Zhari Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his daughters, Miah and Ariauna; his wife, Karyn; his siblings, Robert H., Charity, Jenell, and Natasha; his father, Robert; and his mother, Cynthia Church. Wittman was first posted on crossfit.com on February 10, 2011.

- **Modality:** Kettlebell, barbell, and jumping
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 20-30 minutes
- **Intensity:** Moderate pace per round — grip and hip hinge endurance decide the finish
