# Woehlke

## Description
Honors Detroit firefighter Brian Woehlke. Three rounds, each for time, of barbell work and high-rep gymnastics with 3 minutes of rest between rounds.

```time
(3)
  + 4 Jerks 185lb
  + 5 Front Squats 185lb
  + 6 Power Cleans 185lb
  + 40 Pullups
  + 50 Pushups
  + 60 Situps
  3:00 Rest
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 185-lb barbell |
| Women | 125-lb barbell |

## Profile
Brian Woehlke, 29, of Detroit, Michigan, died at the scene of a fire in Westland, Michigan, on May 8, 2013. A 2008 graduate of the Schoolcraft Fire Program, he joined the Western Wayne Fire Authority in 2012. He is survived by his wife, Jennifer; daughter, Ava; parents, William and Elizabeth; and brothers, William, Robert and Bradley. First posted on crossfit.com on March 29, 2016.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Weightlifting and monostructural gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, repeated intervals with prescribed rest
- **Primary Movements:** Jerk, front squat, power clean, pull-ups, push-ups, sit-ups
- **Intensity:** High — each round is an all-out sprint

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Time of each round
