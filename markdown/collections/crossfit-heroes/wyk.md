# Wyk

## Description
Five rounds of front squats, rope climbs, and a weighted 400-meter plate run.

```time
(5)
  5 Front Squats 225lb
  5 Rope Climbs 15ft
  400m Run 45lb Plate
```

## Profile
Army Pfc. Jacob H. "Wyk" Wykstra, 21, of Thornton, Colorado, was assigned to the 1st Battalion, 12th Infantry Regiment, 4th Brigade Combat Team, 4th Infantry Division. He died on May 28, 2014, in Kandahar Province, Afghanistan, of injuries sustained in an aircraft accident. He is survived by his wife, Katie Wykstra; his mother, Heidi Katzenbach; his father, Thomas Wykstra; his brothers, Aiden and Connor Wykstra; his sister, Hannah Donato; his stepfather, Ray Katzenbach; and his stepmother, Joyce Wykstra. Wyk was first posted on crossfit.com on June 28, 2014.
