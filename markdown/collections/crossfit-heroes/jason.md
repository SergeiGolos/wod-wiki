# Jason

## Description
Honors Navy Special Warfare Operator 1st Class (SEAL) Jason Dale Lewis, killed by an IED in Baghdad in 2007. Squats descend 100-75-50-25 while muscle-ups climb 5-10-15-20 — a trade of legs for rings.

```time
100 Squats
5 Muscle Ups
75 Squats
10 Muscle Ups
50 Squats
15 Muscle Ups
25 Squats
20 Muscle Ups
```

## Profile
Navy Special Warfare Operator 1st Class (SEAL) Jason Dale Lewis was killed by an IED while conducting combat operations in southern Baghdad on July 6, 2007. He is survived by his wife, Donna; and children, Jack, Max, and Grace. The workout was named in honor of his life, family, and courage, first posted on crossfit.com on August 2, 2007.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics with high-volume squats
- **Time Domain:** Moderate-long, typically 15-25 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — 50 ring muscle-ups make this one of the most skill-heavy heroes

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
