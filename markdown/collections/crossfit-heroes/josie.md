# Josie

## Description

A weighted-vest mile sandwiched around three rounds of burpees, power cleans, and front squats, closed with a second mile.

```time
For time:
1 Mile Run 20lb Vest
(3)
  30 Burpees
  4 Power Cleans 155lb
  6 Front Squats 155lb
1 Mile Run 20lb Vest
```

## Scaling

| Level | Power Clean | Front Squat | Vest |
|-------|-------------|-------------|------|
| Men | 155 lb | 155 lb | 20 lb |
| Women | 105 lb | 105 lb | 20 lb |

## Profile

Deputy U.S. Marshal Josie Wells, 27, of Harleston, Mississippi, was killed in the line of duty on March 10, 2015, while serving with the Marshals Service in the Southern District of Mississippi.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Metabolic conditioning and weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Moderate-long, roughly 25-35 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Run, burpee, power clean, front squat
- **Intensity:** High aerobic with barbell sets
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate

## Collectible Metrics
