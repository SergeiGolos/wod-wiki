# CHAD1000X

## Description

One thousand weighted box step-ups with a ruck, created with Sara Wilkinson and Rogue Fitness.

```time
For time:
1000 Weighted Box Step Ups 20in
// wear a 45lb ruck
```

## Scaling

| Level | Ruck | Box |
|-------|------|-----|
| Men | 45 lb | 20 in |
| Women | 35 lb | 20 in |

## Profile

Navy SEAL Senior Chief Chad Wilkinson, who served more than two decades across many deployments, died by suicide on Oct. 29, 2018, from the effects of traumatic brain injuries, blast-wave injuries, and PTSD.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weight-bearing endurance
- **Time Domain:** Very long, 45-90 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Weighted box step-up
- **Intensity:** Sustained, low-skill grind
- **Skill Level:** Beginner

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
