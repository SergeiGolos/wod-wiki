# Ryan

## Description
Honors Maplewood, Missouri, firefighter Ryan Hummert, killed by sniper fire in 2008. Five rounds of 7 muscle-ups and 21 burpees for time.

```time
(5)
  7 Muscle Ups
  21 Burpees
```

## Profile
Ryan Hummert, 22, was a firefighter in Maplewood, Missouri. He was killed by sniper fire on July 21, 2008, when he stepped off his fire truck responding to a call. He is survived by his parents, Andrew and Jackie. Ryan was first posted on crossfit.com on October 8, 2008.

- **Modality:** Gymnastics plus burpees
- **Time Domain:** Moderate-long, typically 15-25 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — 35 muscle-ups and 105 burpees reward staying unbroken early
