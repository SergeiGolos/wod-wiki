# White

## Description
Five rounds of rope climbs, toes-to-bar, overhead walking lunges with a plate, and a 400-meter run.

```time
(5)
  3 Rope Climbs 15ft
  10 Toes to Bar
  21 Overhead Walking Lunges 45lb
  Run 400m
```

## Scaling

| Level | Plate |
|-------|-------|
| Men | 45 lb |
| Women | 25 lb |

## Profile
U.S. Army 1st Lt. Ashley White, 24, 230th Brigade Support Battalion, 30th Brigade Combat Team, N.C. National Guard, died October 22, 2011 of wounds from an IED in Kandahar, Afghanistan.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics, weighted lunge, and running
- **Time Domain:** Long, 30-45 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Rope climb, toes-to-bar, overhead walking lunge, run
- **Intensity:** Grindy; overhead plate lunges tax grip and shoulders

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all five rounds
