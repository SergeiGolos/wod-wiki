# Monti

## Description
Honors Medal of Honor recipient Sgt. 1st Class Jared C. Monti. Five rounds for time alternating 50 barbell step-ups with 15 cleans, then 50 more step-ups and 10 snatches.

```time
(5)
  50 Barbell Step-ups 45lb
  15 Cleans 135lb
  50 Barbell Step-ups 45lb
  10 Snatches 135lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 45-lb barbell, 20-inch box, 135-lb clean and snatch |
| Women | 35-lb barbell, 20-inch box, 95-lb clean and snatch |

## Profile
Sgt. 1st Class Jared C. Monti, 30, of Raynham, Massachusetts, was killed on June 21, 2006, during combat operations in Gowardesh, Afghanistan. Monti was assigned to the 3rd Squadron, 71st Cavalry, 3rd Brigade Combat Team, 10th Mountain Division at Fort Drum, New York. First posted on crossfit.com on July 15, 2016.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Weightlifting with weighted step-ups
- **Time Domain:** Long — 150 step-ups per round pair
- **Primary Movements:** Barbell step-ups, cleans, snatches
- **Intensity:** High — the step-up volume dominates
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (squat-snatch and clean endurance)

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
