# Yeti

## Description
Honors smokejumper Mark "Yeti" Urban. For time: 25 pull-ups, 10 muscle-ups, a 1.5-mile run, 10 more muscle-ups, and 25 more pull-ups.

```time
25 Pullups
10 Muscle-ups
1.5mile Run
10 Muscle-ups

```

## Profile
Mark Thomas Urban, 40, was killed on Sept. 27, 2013, during a parachute jump while conducting an equipment evaluation at a remote airstrip outside of Prairie, Idaho. A Senior Smokejumper with the Bureau of Land Management's Great Basin Smokejumpers, he began his career as a wildland firefighter for the U.S. Forest Service in 1999. First posted on crossfit.com on February 21, 2017.
