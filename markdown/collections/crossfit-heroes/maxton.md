# Maxton

## Description

Thirteen rounds of strict pull-ups, box step-ups, and burpees in a weight vest.

```time
(13)
  8 Strict Pullups
  26 Box Step Ups 24in
  21 Burpees
// wear a 20lb weight vest
```

## Scaling

| Level | Vest | Box |
|-------|------|-----|
| Men | 20 lb | 24 in |
| Women | 14 lb | 20 in |

## Profile

Navy Hospitalman Maxton Soviak, 22, an avid CrossFit athlete among the 13 U.S. service members killed in the attack outside the Kabul airport in Afghanistan on Aug. 26, 2021, was remembered for loving hard work and pulling others up to be great.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Gymnastics and metabolic conditioning
- **Time Domain:** Long, 30-45 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Strict pull-up, box step-up, burpee
- **Intensity:** Sustained grind
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate (strict pull-up volume)

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all thirteen rounds
