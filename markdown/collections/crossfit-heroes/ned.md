# Ned

## Description
Seven rounds of 11 bodyweight back squats and a 1,000-meter row.

```time
(7)
  11 Back Squats 1bw
  1000m Row
```

## Profile
Special Agent Nathan B. "Ned" Schuldheiss, 27, of Newport, Rhode Island, was a civilian assigned to the Air Force Office of Special Investigations, Detachment 204, Offutt Air Force Base, Nebraska. He died on Nov. 1, 2007, near Balad Air Base, Iraq, of wounds sustained from an improvised explosive device that struck his vehicle. He is survived by his father, retired Lt. Col. Jeff Schuldheiss; his mother, Sarah Conlan; his stepfather, Kris Mills; and his sister, Erin Dreeszen. Ned was first posted on crossfit.com on Oct. 30, 2014.
