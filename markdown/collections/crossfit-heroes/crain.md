# Crain

## Profile

Officer Michael "Freight" Crain, 34, an 11-year Riverside Police Department veteran and former Marine sergeant, died February 7, 2013, fatally shot in an apparent ambush while on patrol in Beaumont, California. Two rounds of 34-rep movements, each separated by 50-yard sprints.

```time
(2)
  34 Pushups
  50yd Sprint
  34 Deadlifts 135lb
  50yd Sprint
  34 Box Jumps 24in
  50yd Sprint
  34 Clean and Jerks 95lb
  50yd Sprint
  34 Burpees
  50yd Sprint
  34 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
  50yd Sprint
  34 Pullups
  50yd Sprint
```

## Scaling

| Level | Deadlift | Box | Clean and Jerk | Medicine Ball |
|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| Men | 135 lb | 24 in | 95 lb | 20 lb, 10-ft target |
| Women | 95 lb | 24 in | 65 lb | 14 lb, 9-ft target |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting, gymnastics, and monostructural cardio
- **Time Domain:** Long, no cap
- **Primary Movements:** Push-up, sprint, deadlift, box jump, clean and jerk, burpee, wall-ball shot, pull-up
- **Intensity:** High; sprint speed between movements drives the score

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
