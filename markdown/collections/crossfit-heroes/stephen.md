# Stephen

## Description
Honors Canadian Cpl. Stephen Bouzane, killed by an IED in Afghanistan in 2007. Descending rounds of 30-25-20-15-10-5 across GHD sit-ups, back extensions, knees-to-elbows, and stiff-legged deadlifts at 95 lb.

## Profile
Cpl. Stephen Bouzane, 26, served with 3rd Battalion, Princess Patricia's Canadian Light Infantry. He was killed by an IED on June 20, 2007, in the Panjwaii District of Afghanistan. He is survived by his parents, Fred and Moureen, and sister, Kelly. Stephen was first posted on crossfit.com on February 7, 2010.
