# Walsh

## Description
Four rounds of burpee pull-ups, back squats, and a 200-meter overhead plate run.

```time
(4)
  22 Burpee Pull Ups
  22 Back Squats 185lb
  400m Run 45lb Plate
```

## Profile
U.S. Army 1st Lt. Jonathan P. Walsh, 28, of Cobb, Georgia, was assigned to the 2nd Battalion, 504th Infantry, 1st Brigade Combat Team, 82nd Airborne Division, based at Fort Bragg, North Carolina. He died on April 22, 2012, in Paktia, Afghanistan, when enemy forces attacked his unit with an improvised explosive device. He is survived by his wife, Debbra; his son, Austin; his parents, Carolyn and Paul; and his brother, Christopher. Walsh was first posted on crossfit.com on Oct. 20, 2013.
