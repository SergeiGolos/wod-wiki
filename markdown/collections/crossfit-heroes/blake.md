# Blake

## Description
Honors Navy Senior Chief Cryptologic Technician David Blake McLendon, killed in a helicopter crash in Zabul Province, Afghanistan, in 2010. Four rounds of overhead walking lunges, box jumps, wall-ball shots, and handstand push-ups for time.

```time
(4)
  100ft Overhead Walking Lunges 45lb
  30 Box Jumps 24in
  20 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
  10 Handstand Push Ups
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 45-lb plate, 24-inch box, 20-lb medicine ball to a 10-foot target |
| Women | 25-lb plate, 20-inch box, 14-lb medicine ball to a 9-foot target |

## Profile
Navy Senior Chief Cryptologic Technician (Technical) David Blake McLendon, 30, of Thomasville, Georgia, was assigned to Naval Special Warfare Group 2 Support Activity in Norfolk, Virginia. He was killed on September 21, 2010, in a helicopter crash during combat operations in Zabul Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his wife, Kate; his parents, David and Mary-Ann; his brother, Chris; and his sister, Kelly Lockman. Blake was first posted on crossfit.com on November 13, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Overhead carrying, jumping, and gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 20-30 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — the plate lunge and handstand push-ups tax the same shoulders

## Collectible Metrics
