# Viola

## Description
Honors Army Staff Sgt. Alex Viola. A 20-minute AMRAP of a 400-meter run, 11 power snatches, 17 pull-ups, and 13 power cleans.

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  400m Run
  11 Power Snatches 95lb
  17 Pullups
  13 Power Cleans 95lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 95-lb barbell |
| Women | 65-lb barbell |

## Profile
U.S. Army Staff Sgt. Alex Viola, 29, originally of Keller, Texas, was killed Nov. 17, 2013, in Kandahar Province, Afghanistan, from wounds caused by an improvised explosive device. An engineer, he was assigned to the 3rd Battalion, 7th Special Forces Group at Eglin Air Force Base, Florida. First posted on crossfit.com on November 11, 2017.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Monostructural and barbell cycling with gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Fixed, 20-minute AMRAP
- **Primary Movements:** 400-meter runs, power snatches, pull-ups, power cleans
- **Intensity:** High — shoulder fatigue compounds each round

## Collectible Metrics
- **Reps:** Total rounds and reps completed
