# Michael

## Description
Honors Navy Lt. Michael McGreevy, a SEAL killed in Afghanistan in 2005. Three rounds of an 800-meter run, 50 back extensions, and 50 sit-ups make a long, monostructural stamina test.

```time
(3)
  800m Run
  50 Back Extensions
  50 Sit Ups
```

## Profile
Navy Lt. Michael McGreevy, 30, of Portville, New York, was a U.S. Navy SEAL. He was killed in Afghanistan on June 28, 2005. He is survived by his wife, Laura, and 14-month-old daughter, Molly. Michael was first posted on crossfit.com on July 15, 2005.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Monostructural endurance plus midline stamina
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 20-30 minutes
- **Intensity:** Sustained aerobic — the back and hip flexors are the limiting tissue

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
