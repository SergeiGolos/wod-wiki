# Bradley

## Description
Ten rounds of double 100-meter sprints bracketing pull-ups and burpees, with 30 seconds of rest each round.

```time
(10)
  Sprint 100m
  10 Pullups
  Sprint 100m
  10 Burpees
  0:30 Rest
```

## Profile
U.S. Air Force Senior Airman Bradley R. Smith, 24, 10th Air Support Operations Squadron, killed by an IED January 3, 2010 in Zhari District, Kandahar Province, Afghanistan.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Sprinting and bodyweight gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, about 20 minutes including rest
- **Primary Movements:** 100-meter sprint, pull-up, burpee
- **Intensity:** Repeat-sprint; all-out efforts on short recovery

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time including prescribed rest periods
