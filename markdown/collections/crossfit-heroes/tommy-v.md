# Tommy V

## Description
Honors Navy Senior Chief Petty Officer Thomas J. Valentine, killed in a training accident in 2008. Descending sets of thrusters at 115 lb paired with rope climbs — 21/12, 15/9, 9/6 — for time.

```time
21 Thrusters 115lb
12 Rope Climbs, 15 ft
15 Thrusters 115lb
9 Rope Climbs, 15 ft
9 Thrusters 115lb
6 Rope Climbs, 15 ft
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | Thrusters 115 lb, 15-foot rope |
| Women | Thrusters 75 lb, 15-foot rope |

## Profile
Navy Senior Chief Petty Officer Thomas J. Valentine, 37, of Ham Lake, Minnesota, was a U.S. Navy SEAL. He died in a training accident in Arizona on February 13, 2008. He is survived by his wife, Christina; son, John; and daughter, Meghan. Tommy V was first posted on crossfit.com on March 12, 2008.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Barbell strength plus rope climbs
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, typically 10-20 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — thrusters before every set of climbs tax the pulling grip

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
