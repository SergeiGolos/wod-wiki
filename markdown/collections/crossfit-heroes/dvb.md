# DVB

## Description
Honors Las Vegas police officer David Vanbuskirk. Runs with a medicine bookend descending rounds of wall-ball shots and rope climbs: 8, then 4, then 2.

```time
1mile Run 20lb
(8)
  10 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
  1 Rope Climb
800m Run 20lb
(4)
  10 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
  1 Rope Climb
400m Run 20lb
(2)
  10 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
  1 Rope Climb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 20-lb medicine ball to a 10-foot target |
| Women | 14-lb medicine ball to a 9-foot target |

## Profile
Officer David Vanbuskirk, 36, of Henderson, Nevada, was killed on July 23, 2013, when he fell during a nighttime aerial rescue mission. First posted on crossfit.com on August 26, 2016.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Monostructural, gymnastics, and weighted ball work
- **Time Domain:** Long — 3-plus miles of running with a medicine ball
- **Primary Movements:** Medicine-ball running, wall-ball shots, rope climbs
- **Intensity:** High — grip and midline fatigue accumulate
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (rope climbs while fatigued)

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
