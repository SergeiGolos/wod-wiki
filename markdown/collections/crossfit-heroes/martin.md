# Martin

## Description

A partner chipper alternating rowing distances with high-rep sets of deadlifts, thrusters, push-ups, pull-ups, sit-ups, and wall-ball shots.

```time
For time, with a partner:
2000m Row
100 Deadlifts Bodyweight
50 Thrusters 95lb
1000m Row
100 Hand Release Pushups
50 Pullups
500m Row
100 Situps
100 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Thruster | Wall Ball | Deadlift |
|-------|----------|-----------|----------|
| Men | 95 lb | 20 lb | Bodyweight |
| Women | 65 lb | 14 lb | Bodyweight |

## Profile

British Army Pvt. Martin Simon George Bell of the 2nd Battalion, Parachute Regiment, a former Police Community Support Officer in West Yorkshire, was killed by an IED on Jan. 25, 2011, near Spoor Kalay in Helmand Province, Afghanistan, while moving to assist a wounded comrade.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Metabolic conditioning, weightlifting, and gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Long, 25-40 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Row, deadlift, thruster, hand-release push-up, pull-up, sit-up, wall-ball shot
- **Intensity:** Sustained, high volume
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate

## Collectible Metrics
