# Brenton

## Description
Honors Seattle Police Officer Timothy Brenton, shot and killed in a drive-by shooting while on duty in 2009. Five rounds of a 100-foot bear crawl and 100 feet of standing broad jumps — three burpees after every five jumps.

```time
(5)
  100ft Bear Crawl
  100ft Standing Broad Jump
```

## Profile
Seattle Police Department Field Training Officer Timothy Quinn Brenton, 39, was shot and killed in a drive-by shooting while on duty on October 31, 2009. He is survived by his wife, Lisa; his son, Quinn; and his daughter, Kayliegh. Brenton was first posted on crossfit.com on October 21, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Bodyweight locomotion and jumping
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 20-30 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — burpee-laden broad jumps leave the legs shaking for the next crawl

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
