# Rocket

## Description
A 30-minute swim-and-calisthenics AMRAP of a 50-yard swim, push-ups, and squats.

```time
30:00 AMRAP
  50yd Swim
  10 Push Ups
  15 Squats
```

## Profile
U.S. Army Sgt. 1st Class Aaron "Rocket" Henderson, 33, of Houlton, Maine, was assigned to the 2nd Battalion, 5th Special Forces Group (Airborne). He died on Oct. 2, 2012, at Bagram Air Base, Afghanistan, of wounds suffered from an improvised explosive device in Zombalay Village, Afghanistan. He is survived by his mother, Christine; his brothers, Bob, Corey, and Sam; and many nieces and nephews. He was preceded in death by his father, Dallas. Rocket was first posted on crossfit.com on Sept. 6, 2014.
