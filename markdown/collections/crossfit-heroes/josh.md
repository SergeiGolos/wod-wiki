# Josh

## Description
Honors Army Staff Sgt. Joshua Hager, killed by an IED in Ramadi, Iraq, in 2007. Descending sets of overhead squats at 95 lb paired with double pull-ups — 21-42, 15-30, 9-18 — a grip-and-overhead grind.

```time
21 Overhead Squats 95lb
42 Pull Ups
15 Overhead Squats 95lb
30 Pull Ups
9 Overhead Squats 95lb
18 Pull Ups
```

## Profile
Army Staff Sgt. Joshua Hager, 29, of Broomfield, Colorado, served with 1st Battalion, 9th Infantry Regiment, 2nd Brigade Combat Team, 2nd Infantry Division at Fort Carson. He was killed on February 22, 2007, when an IED detonated near his Humvee during combat operations in Ramadi, Iraq. He is survived by his wife, Heather; son, Bayley; and his parents. Josh was first posted on crossfit.com on February 26, 2007.

- **Time Domain:** Moderate, typically 8-15 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — grip and shoulder fatigue compound across every round
