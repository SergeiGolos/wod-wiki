# DT

## Description
Honors Air Force Staff Sgt. Timothy P. Davis, killed by an IED in Afghanistan in 2009. Five rounds of 12 deadlifts, 9 hang power cleans, and 6 push jerks at 155 lb for time — 135 total barbell reps at 155/105.

```time
(5)
  12 Deadlifts 155lb
  9 Hang Power Cleans 155lb
  6 Push Jerks 155lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 155 lb barbell |
| Women | 105 lb barbell |

## Profile
Air Force Staff Sgt. Timothy P. Davis, 28, was killed on February 20, 2009, in Afghanistan while supporting Operation Enduring Freedom when his vehicle was struck by an IED. He is survived by his wife, Megan, and 1-year-old son, T.J. DT was first posted on crossfit.com on April 14, 2009, and has become a CrossFit Games staple.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Barbell couplet complex
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, typically 8-15 minutes
- **Intensity:** Very high — unbroken rounds are the intended stimulus until they no longer are

## Collectible Metrics
