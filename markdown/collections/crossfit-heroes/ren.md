# René

## Profile

Danish Army Sgt. René Brink Jakobsen, 39, of the elite Jægerkorpset ranger unit, died January 3, 2013, after an improvised explosive device struck his foot patrol in Helmand Province, Afghanistan. Seven rounds of running, walking lunges, pull-ups, and burpees in a weight vest if available.

```time
(7)
  400m Run
  21 Walking Lunges
  15 Pullups
  9 Burpees, wear a 20lb weight vest
```

| Level | Weight Vest |

| Men | 20 lb |
| Women | 14 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Monostructural cardio and gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Long, no cap
- **Primary Movements:** Run, walking lunge, pull-up, burpee
- **Intensity:** High; the vest compounds every element
