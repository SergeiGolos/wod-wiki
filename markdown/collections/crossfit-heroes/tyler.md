# Tyler

## Profile
Army 1st Lt. Tyler E. Parten, 24, of Arkansas, served with 3rd Squadron, 61st Cavalry Regiment, 4th Brigade Combat Team, 4th Infantry Division at Fort Carson, Colorado. He died on September 10, 2009, in Konar Province, Afghanistan, of wounds sustained when insurgents attacked his unit with rocket-propelled grenades and small-arms fire. He is survived by his mother, Lona, and brother, Daniel. Tyler was first posted on crossfit.com on October 17, 2009.
