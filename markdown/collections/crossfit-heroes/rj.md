# RJ

## Description
Honors LAPD officer and Marine Corps Reserve Sgt. Maj. Robert J. Cottle, killed by an IED in southern Afghanistan in 2010. Five rounds of an 800-meter run, rope climbs, and push-ups for time.

```time
(5)
  800m Run
  5 Rope Climbs 15ft
  50 Push Ups
```

## Profile
LAPD officer and U.S. Marine Corps Reserve Sgt. Maj. Robert J. Cottle, 45, was killed by an improvised explosive device while on patrol in southern Afghanistan on March 24, 2010. He joined the Marines at 18 and the LAPD in 1990, serving in Hollywood Vice, Southeast Area, the Dive Team, and SWAT. He is survived by his wife, Emily, and 9-month-old daughter, Kaila. RJ was first posted on crossfit.com on June 22, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Running, climbing, and pushing
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 30-40 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — 25 rope climbs and 250 push-ups pile on after every half-mile

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
