# Whitten

## Description
Honors Army Capt. Dan Whitten, killed when his vehicle was struck by an IED in Zabul, Afghanistan, in 2010. Five rounds of 22 kettlebell swings, 22 box jumps, a 400-meter run, 22 burpees, and 22 wall-ball shots for time.

```time
(5)
  22 KB Swings 72lb
  22 Box Jumps 24in
  400m Run
  22 Burpees
  22 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|

| Women | 53-lb kettlebell, 20-inch box, 14-lb medicine ball to a 9-foot target |

## Profile
Army Capt. Dan Whitten, 28, of Grimes, Iowa, was assigned to the 1st Battalion, 508th Parachute Infantry Regiment, 4th Brigade Combat Team, 82nd Airborne Division, based at Fort Bragg, North Carolina. He died on February 2, 2010, when enemy forces in Zabul, Afghanistan, attacked his vehicle with an improvised explosive device. He is survived by his wife, Starr; his mother, Jill; his father, Dan; and his sister, Army Capt. Sarah Whitten. Whitten was first posted on crossfit.com on December 12, 2010.

- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 30-45 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — the 22s pile burpees and wall balls onto legs already run down
