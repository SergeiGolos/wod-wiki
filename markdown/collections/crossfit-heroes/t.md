# T

## Description

Five sprint-led rounds of squat clean thrusters and kettlebell swings, resting two minutes between rounds.

```time
(5)
  100m Sprint
  10 Squat Clean Thrusters 115lb
  15 KB Swings 53lb
  100m Sprint
  2:00 Rest
```

## Scaling

| Level | Squat Clean Thruster | Kettlebell Swing |
|-------|----------------------|------------------|
| Men | 115 lb | 53 lb |
| Women | 75 lb | 35 lb |

## Profile

Marine Master Sgt. Aaron Torian, 36, of Paducah, Kentucky, a member of 2nd Marine Special Operations Battalion on his sixth deployment, died Feb. 15, 2014, during combat operations in Helmand Province, Afghanistan.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Metabolic conditioning and weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Short intervals, about 15-20 minutes with rests
- **Primary Movements:** Sprint, squat clean thruster, kettlebell swing
- **Intensity:** Very high per round
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete all five rounds
