# Holbrook

## Description
Honors Army Capt. Jason Holbrook, Special Forces soldier killed when his vehicle struck an IED in Tsagay, Afghanistan, in 2010. Ten rounds, each for time, of thrusters, pull-ups, and a 100-meter sprint, resting one minute between rounds.

```time
(10)
  5 Thrusters 115lb
  10 Pull Ups
  100m Sprint
  1:00 Rest
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 115-lb barbell |
| Women | 75-lb barbell |

## Profile
Army Capt. Jason "Rat" Holbrook, 28, of Burnet, Texas, was assigned to the 1st Battalion, 3rd Special Forces Group (Airborne), based at Fort Bragg, North Carolina. He was killed on July 29, 2010, in Tsagay, Afghanistan, when insurgents attacked his vehicle with an improvised explosive device. He is survived by his wife, Heather, and his parents, Joan and James. Holbrook was first posted on crossfit.com on January 27, 2011.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Barbell, gymnastics, and sprinting
- **Time Domain:** Interval effort, typically 25-35 minutes including rest
- **Intensity:** Sprint each round — the thrusters load the legs for every pull-up and dash

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete (rest included)
