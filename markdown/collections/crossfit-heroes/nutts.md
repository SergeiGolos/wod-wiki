# Nutts

## Description
Honors Canadian Lt. Andrew Nuttall of the Princess Patricia's Canadian Light Infantry, killed by an IED in Afghanistan in 2009. A long chipper of handstand push-ups, heavy deadlifts, box jumps, pull-ups, wall-ball shots, double-unders, and a weighted run.

```time
10 Handstand Push Ups
15 Deadlifts 250lb
25 Box Jumps 30in
50 Pull Ups
100 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
200 Double Unders
400m Run 45lb
```

## Scaling

| Women | 175-lb deadlifts, 24-inch box, 14-lb medicine ball to 9 feet, 25-lb plate |

## Profile
Canadian Armed Forces Lt. Andrew Richard Nuttall, 30, of 1st Battalion, Princess Patricia's Canadian Light Infantry, based in Edmonton, Alberta, was killed by an IED during a joint foot patrol near the village of Nakhonay in Panjwaii District, Afghanistan, on December 23, 2009. He is survived by his parents, Richard and Ethel Jane. Nutts was first posted on crossfit.com on May 28, 2010.
