# Havana

## Description

A 25-minute triplet of double-unders, push-ups, and power cleans.

```time
25:00 AMRAP
  150 Double Unders
  50 Pushups
  15 Power Cleans 185lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Power Clean |
|-------|-------------|
| Men | 185 lb |
| Women | 125 lb |

## Profile

Italian Army Corpo Legionario Maggiore Scelto Roberto Marchini, 28, of Viterbo, Italy, an 8th Airborne Combat Engineer Regiment (Folgore Brigade) soldier who trained CrossFit with improvised equipment, died during a reconnaissance mission in Afghanistan's Bakwa district on July 12, 2011.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Metabolic conditioning and weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Long, 25-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Double-under, push-up, power clean
- **Intensity:** Sustained moderate-high
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate (double-under volume)

## Collectible Metrics

- **Reps:** Rounds and reps completed
