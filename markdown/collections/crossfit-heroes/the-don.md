# The Don

## Description
A 66-rep chipper of ten movements running from deadlifts through double-unders.

```time
66 Deadlifts 110lb
66 Box Jumps 24in
66 Kettlebell Swings 53lb
66 Knees To Elbows
66 Sit Ups
66 Pull Ups
66 Thrusters 55lb
66 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
66 Burpees
66 Double Unders
```

## Profile
U.S. Marine Cpl. Donald M. Marler, 22, of St. Louis, Missouri, was assigned to the 3rd Battalion, 1st Marine Regiment, 1st Marine Division, I Marine Expeditionary Force, based at Camp Pendleton, California. He died on June 6, 2010, while supporting combat operations in Helmand Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his mother, Susan; his father, David Sr.; his sister, Jennifer Pupillo; and his brothers, David Jr. and Jacob. The Don was first posted on crossfit.com on Sept. 11, 2013.

- **Modality:** Mixed modal chipper
