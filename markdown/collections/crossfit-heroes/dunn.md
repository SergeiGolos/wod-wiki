# Dunn

## Description
Honors Marine Lance Cpl. Kielin Dunn. A 19-minute AMRAP of 3 muscle-ups, a 5-10-15-yard shuttle sprint, and 6 burpee box jump-overs cleared without touching the box.

```time
19:00 AMRAP
  3 Muscle-ups
  1 Shuttle Sprint (5-10-15 yards)
  6 Burpee Box Jump-overs 20in
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 20-inch box |
| Women | 20-inch box |

## Profile
Marine Corps Lance Cpl. Kielin Dunn, 19, of Chesapeake, Virginia, died on Feb. 18, 2010, while supporting combat operations in Helmand Province, Afghanistan. He was assigned to the 1st Battalion, 6th Marine Regiment, 2nd Marine Division, II Marine Expeditionary Force. First posted on crossfit.com on August 24, 2017.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics and sprint work
- **Time Domain:** Fixed, 19-minute AMRAP
- **Primary Movements:** Muscle-ups, shuttle sprints, burpee box jump-overs
- **Intensity:** High — jumping demands as skill wanes
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (muscle-ups under fatigue)
