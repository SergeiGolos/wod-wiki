# Forrest

## Description
Honors DEA Special Agent Forrest Leamon, killed when his helicopter crashed on a counternarcotics mission in western Afghanistan in 2009. Three rounds of L pull-ups, toes-to-bars, burpees, and an 800-meter run for time.

```time
(3)
  20 L Pull Ups
  30 Toes To Bar
  40 Burpees
  800m Run
```

## Profile
Drug Enforcement Administration Special Agent Forrest Nelson Leamon, 37, was assigned to the Foreign-deployed Advisory and Support Team (FAST) Echo. He was killed on October 26, 2009, when his helicopter crashed while on a counternarcotics mission in western Afghanistan. He is survived by his wife, Ana; his son, Luke; his parents, Sue and Richard; and his sister, Heather. Forrest was first posted on crossfit.com on October 17, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Midline gymnastics and running
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 25-35 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — L pull-ups and toes-to-bars hollow out the trunk before each run

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
