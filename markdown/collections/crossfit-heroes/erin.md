# Erin

## Description
Honors Canadian Army Master Cpl. Erin Doyle, killed in Afghanistan in 2008. Five rounds of 15 dumbbell split cleans at 40 lb and 21 pull-ups for time.

```time
(5)
  15 DB Split Cleans 40lb
  21 Pull Ups
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | Dumbbell split cleans 40 lb |
| Women | Dumbbell split cleans 30 lb |

## Profile
Canadian Army Master Cpl. Erin Doyle, 32, was killed in a firefight on August 11, 2008, in the Panjwaii District of Kandahar Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his wife, Nicole, and daughter, Zarine. Erin was first posted on crossfit.com on October 9, 2008.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Dumbbell strength plus pulling gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, typically 10-20 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — grip demand stacks across every dumbbell-and-pull-up round

## Collectible Metrics
