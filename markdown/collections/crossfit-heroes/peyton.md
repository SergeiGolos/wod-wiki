# Peyton

## Description

A 20-minute couplet AMRAP interrupted by double-unders every two minutes, followed by a two-mile run.

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  10 Chest-to-Bar Pullups
  10 DB Thrusters 50lb
// every 2 minutes, including 0:00: stop and do 40 Double Unders
Then:
2 Mile Run
```

## Scaling

| Level | Dumbbells |
|-------|-----------|
| Men | 50 lb pair |
| Women | 35 lb pair |

## Profile

Chad Robert Peyton, a U.S. Army captain and UH-60 Black Hawk pilot who served in Operation Iraqi Freedom II and earned the Bronze Star, died by suicide on May 17, 2021, after struggling with depression and anxiety.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Gymnastics, weightlifting, and metabolic conditioning
- **Time Domain:** Long, 20-minute AMRAP plus run
- **Primary Movements:** Chest-to-bar pull-up, dumbbell thruster, double-under, run
- **Intensity:** High, paced around the clock interruptions
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (chest-to-bar volume)

## Collectible Metrics

- **Reps:** Rounds and reps of the AMRAP, plus run time
