# Bull

## Description
Honors Marine Capt. Brandon "Bull" Barrett, killed in Helmand Province, Afghanistan, in 2010. Two rounds of 200 double-unders, 50 overhead squats, 50 pull-ups, and a one-mile run for time.

```time
(2)
  200 Double Unders
  50 Overhead Squats 135lb
  50 Pull Ups
  1 Mile Run
```

## Profile
Marine Capt. Brandon "Bull" Barrett, 27, of Marion, Indiana, was assigned to the 1st Battalion, 6th Marine Regiment, 2nd Marine Division, II Marine Expeditionary Force, based at Camp Lejeune, North Carolina. He was killed on May 5, 2010, while supporting combat operations in Helmand Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his parents, Cindy and Brett; his sisters, Ashley and Taylor; his brother, Brock; and his grandmother, Carmen Johnson. Bull was first posted on crossfit.com on December 26, 2010.
