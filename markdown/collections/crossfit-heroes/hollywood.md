# Hollywood

## Profile
Sgt. Jonathan Stuart "Hollywood" Hollingsworth, 35, of Rotherham, Yorkshire, England, was killed on Nov. 23, 2006, during a mission in Basra, Iraq, while serving with D Squadron. First posted on crossfit.com on November 22, 2016.

- **Skill Level:** Advanced (22 muscle-ups)
