## Profile
U.S. Air Force Senior Airman Bryan R. Bell, 23, of Erie, Pennsylvania, was assigned to the 2nd Civil Engineer Squadron at Barksdale Air Force Base, Louisiana. He died on Jan. 5, 2012, at Camp Bastion in Afghanistan, of injuries suffered when his vehicle struck an improvised explosive device. He is survived by his wife, Alaina; his parents, Richard Bell and Brenda Hart; and a large extended family. Bell was first posted on crossfit.com on July 4, 2014.
