# Abbate

## Description
Honors Marine Sgt. Matthew Abbate, killed in combat operations in Helmand Province, Afghanistan, in 2010. A 21 Clean & Jerks sandwiched between 800 meters and a mile of running — bookended by a mile on each end.

```time
1 Mile Run
21 Clean & Jerks 155lb
800m Run
21 Clean & Jerks 155lb
1 Mile Run
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 155-lb barbell |
| Women | 105-lb barbell |

## Profile
Marine Sgt. Matthew T. Abbate, 26, of Honolulu, Hawaii, was assigned to the 3rd Battalion, 5th Marine Regiment, 1st Marine Division, I Marine Expeditionary Force, based at Camp Pendleton, California. He was killed on December 2, 2010, while conducting combat operations in Helmand Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his wife, Stacie Rigall; his son, Carson; his mother, Karen Binion; his father, Salvatore; and his siblings, Dominica, Elliot, Valerie Binion, and Kelly Binion. Abbate was first posted on crossfit.com on March 26, 2011.
