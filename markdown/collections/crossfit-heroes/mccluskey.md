# McCluskey

## Description
Honors Army Sgt. Jason "Mick" McCluskey, killed by insurgent small-arms fire in Afghanistan in 2010. Three rounds of muscle-ups, burpee pull-ups, pull-ups, and an 800-meter run; wear a 20-lb vest or body armor if you have one.

```time
(3)
  9 Muscle Ups
  15 Burpee Pull Ups
  21 Pull Ups
  800m Run
```

## Profile
Army Sgt. Jason "Mick" McCluskey, 26, of McAlester, Oklahoma, was assigned to the 27th Engineer Battalion, 20th Engineer Brigade, XVIII Airborne Corps, based at Fort Bragg, North Carolina. He was killed on November 4, 2010, when insurgents attacked his unit with small-arms fire in Zarghun Shahr, Mohammad Agha District, Afghanistan. He is survived by his son, Landen, and his mother, Delores Olivares. McCluskey was first posted on crossfit.com on March 1, 2011.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Upper-body gymnastics and running
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 20-30 minutes
- **Intensity:** Grip-searing — burpee pull-ups and high-rep pull-ups compound round after round
