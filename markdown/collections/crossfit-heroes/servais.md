# Servais

## Profile

Air Force Senior Airman Adam Servais, 23, of the 23rd Special Tactics Squadron at Hurlburt Field, Florida, died August 19, 2006, when his vehicle came under hostile fire in Uruzgan Province, Afghanistan. A 1.5-mile run, eight 19-rep rounds, then weighted sandbag and farmers carries.

```time
Run 1.5 miles, then (8)
  19 Pullups
  19 Pushups
  19 Burpees
400m Sandbag Carry
1-mile Farmers Carry 45lb dumbbells
```

## Scaling

| Level | Farmers Carry |
|-------|-------|
| Men | 45-lb dumbbells |
| Women | 30-lb dumbbells |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Gymnastics, monostructural cardio, and loaded carry
- **Time Domain:** Very long, no cap
- **Primary Movements:** Run, pull-up, push-up, burpee, sandbag carry, farmers carry
- **Intensity:** High; grip endurance decides the carries
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (carry and high-volume calisthenics endurance)

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
