# Roy

## Description
Honors Marine Sgt. Michael C. Roy, MARSOC operator killed in Nimroz Province, Afghanistan, in 2009. Five rounds of 15 deadlifts, 20 box jumps, and 25 pull-ups for time.

```time
(5)
  15 Deadlifts 225lb
  20 Box Jumps 24in
  25 Pull Ups
```

| Women | 155-lb deadlifts, 20-inch box |

## Profile
Marine Sgt. Michael C. Roy, 25, of North Fort Myers, Florida, was assigned to the 3rd Marine Special Operations Battalion, Marine Special Operations Advisor Group, Marine Corps Forces Special Operations Command at Camp Lejeune, North Carolina. He was killed in action on July 8, 2009, in Nimroz Province, Afghanistan, while supporting combat operations. He is survived by his wife, Amy, and three children, Michael, Landon, and Olivia. Roy was first posted on crossfit.com on July 27, 2010.
