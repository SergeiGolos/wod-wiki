# Scooter

## Profile

Sgt. Scott Lunger, 48, a Hayward Police Department veteran since 2001 and SWAT member, died July 22, 2015, after being gunned down during a traffic stop in Brentwood, California. A partner workout on a 35-minute clock: a 30-minute AMRAP, then 5 minutes to find a one-rep-max partner deadlift; solo, rest 60 seconds between rounds and deadlift solo.

```time
On a 35:00 clock with a partner, 30:00 AMRAP:
  30 Double Unders
  15 Pullups
  15 Pushups
  100m Sprint
Then 5:00 to find a 1RM Partner Deadlift
```

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Gymnastics, monostructural cardio, and weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Moderate-long, 35-minute total clock
- **Primary Movements:** Double-under, pull-up, push-up, sprint, partner deadlift
- **Intensity:** High; partners alternate full rounds

## Collectible Metrics

- **Reps:** AMRAP rounds and reps completed
- **Weight:** Partner deadlift 1RM
