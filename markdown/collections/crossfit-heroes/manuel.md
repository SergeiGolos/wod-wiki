# Manuel

## Description
Honors Italian Army Capt. Manuel Fiorito. Five rounds on the clock of timed rope climbs, squats, push-ups, and a 400-meter run, wearing a weight vest or body armor.

```time
20lb Weight Vest
(5)
  3:00 ? Rope Climbs
  2:00 ? Squats
  2:00 ? Pushups
  3:00 400m Run
```

## Profile
Italian Army Capt. Manuel Fiorito, 27, of Verona, Italy, was killed May 5, 2006, when a roadside bomb detonated near his vehicle in Kabul, Afghanistan. Shortly before he died, Fiorito tended to other wounded men and prepared for defense against the attack. First posted on crossfit.com on December 2, 2016.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Gymnastics and monostructural work in intervals
- **Time Domain:** Fixed — 10 minutes of timed intervals per round set
- **Primary Movements:** Rope climbs, squats, push-ups, 400-meter runs
- **Intensity:** High — rest is only what remains of each 3-minute run window
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (weighted rope climbs)

## Collectible Metrics
- **Reps:** Rope climbs, squats, and push-ups completed each round
