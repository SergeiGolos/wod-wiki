# Bowen

## Profile

Capt. Jeffrey Bowen, 37, a 13-year veteran of the Asheville Fire Department assigned to Rescue 3, died July 28, 2011, from injuries suffered fighting a four-alarm fire in a medical building. Three rounds of running, deadlifts, burpee pull-ups, single-arm kettlebell thrusters, and box jumps.
