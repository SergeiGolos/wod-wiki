# Morrison

## Description
A descending 50-40-30-20-10 rep scheme of wall-ball shots, box jumps, and kettlebell swings.

```time
(50-40-30-20-10)
  Wall Ball Shots 20lb
  Box Jumps 24in
  Kettlebell Swings 53lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Wall Ball | Box | Kettlebell |
|-------|-----------|-----|------------|
| Men | 20 lb to 10 ft | 24 in | 53 lb |
| Women | 14 lb to 9 ft | 20 in | 35 lb |

## Profile
U.S. Army Spc. Scott Morrison, 23, 584th Mobility Augmentation Company, 20th Engineer Battalion, died September 26, 2010 from injuries in an IED attack on his vehicle in Kandahar, Afghanistan.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Mixed-modal conditioning
- **Time Domain:** Long, 25-40 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Wall-ball shot, box jump, kettlebell swing
- **Intensity:** Grindy; 150 reps per movement punish sloppy pacing

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to finish the ladder
