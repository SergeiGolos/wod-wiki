# Willy

## Description
Three rounds of three runs anchored by front squats, chest-to-bar pull-ups, and kettlebell swings.

```time
(3)
  800m Run
  5 Front Squats 225lb
  200m Run
  11 Chest To Bar Pull Ups
  400m Run
  12 Kettlebell Swings 70lb
```

## Profile
U.S. Marine Sgt. Wade D. Wilson, 22, of Normangee, Texas, was assigned to the 2nd Battalion, 5th Marine Regiment, 1st Marine Division, I Marine Expeditionary Force, based at Camp Pendleton, California. He died on May 11, 2012, while conducting combat operations in Helmand Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his mother and stepfather, Cindy Lee and Ward Easterling; his father and stepmother, Mitchell Boyd and Tammy; his brothers, Chad, Alex, and Curtis; and his sister, Layne. Willy was first posted on crossfit.com on Nov. 22, 2013.
