# Mr. Joshua

## Description
Honors Navy Special Warfare Operator 1st Class (SEAL) Joshua Thomas Harris, killed in Afghanistan in 2008. Five rounds of a 400-meter run, 30 GHD sit-ups, and 15 deadlifts at 250 lb for time.

```time
(5)
  400m Run
  30 GHD Sit Ups
  15 Deadlifts 250lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | Deadlifts 250 lb |
| Women | Deadlifts 175 lb |

## Profile
Navy Special Warfare Operator 1st Class (SEAL) Joshua Thomas Harris, 36, drowned during combat operations in Afghanistan on August 30, 2008. He is survived by his parents, Dr. Sam and Evelyn; brother, Ranchor; and twin sister, Kiki. Mr. Joshua was first posted on crossfit.com on October 10, 2008.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Monostructural running, midline, heavy barbell
- **Time Domain:** Long, typically 20-30 minutes
- **Intensity:** High — GHD volume plus heavy deadlifts punish the posterior chain

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
