# War Frank

## Description
Honors Marine Capt. Warren A. Frank, killed in Iraq in 2008. Three rounds of 25 muscle-ups, 100 squats, and 35 GHD sit-ups — 75 muscle-ups in a single workout.

```time
(3)
  25 Muscle Ups
  100 Squats
  35 GHD Sit Ups
```

## Profile
Marine Capt. Warren A. Frank, 26, of Cincinnati, Ohio, served with the 5th Air Naval Gunfire Liaison Company, III Marine Expeditionary Force, Okinawa, Japan. He died on November 25, 2008, while supporting combat operations in Ninewa Province, Iraq. He is survived by his wife, Allison, and daughters, Sophia Lynn and Isabella Grace. War Frank was first posted on crossfit.com on March 1, 2010.
