# Locke

## Description

Row-or-run intervals around seven rounds of deadlifts, push-ups, and box step-ups, released March 6, 2026 alongside the companion hero workouts listed below.

```time
For time:
565m Row
(7)
  9 Deadlifts 185lb
  25 Pushups
  21 Box Step Ups 24in
565m Row
// row or run the 565m
// ♀ 125lb, 20in box
```

## Scaling

| Level | Deadlift | Box |
|-------|----------|-----|
| Men | 185 lb | 24 in |
| Women | 125 lb | 20 in |

## Profile

Matthew S. Locke, an Army National Guard veteran of Afghanistan and veteran law-enforcement officer with Tennessee's McNairy County, Adamsville, and Hardin County agencies, died Sept. 25, 2021, while trying to save someone's life. His workout leads a group released in memory of the four Night Stalker crew members — Kraus, Scott, Good, and Cully — killed when their MH-60M Black Hawk crashed during training near Joint Base Lewis-McChord, Washington, on Sept. 17, 2025.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting, gymnastics, and metabolic conditioning
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, roughly 15-25 minutes; the companion sections below are standalone hero workouts of varying length
- **Primary Movements:** Row, deadlift, push-up, box step-up
- **Intensity:** Sustained moderate-high
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate
