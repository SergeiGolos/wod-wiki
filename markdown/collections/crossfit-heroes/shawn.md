# Shawn

## Profile

U.S. Army Capt. Shawn G. Hogan, 28, of the 5th Special Forces Group, died October 17, 2012, in a training exercise at Land Between the Lakes, Kentucky. Five miles of running broken into 5-minute intervals, each followed by 50 squats and 50 push-ups.

```time
Run 5 miles. Every 5:00, stop and perform 50 Squats and 50 Pushups
```

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Monostructural cardio and gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Very long, roughly 40-50 minutes
- **Primary Movements:** Run, squat, push-up
- **Intensity:** Moderate; interval discipline over sprint pace

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
