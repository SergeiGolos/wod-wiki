# Johnson

## Description
Honors Marine 1st Lt. Michael E. Johnson, killed supporting combat operations in Kunar Province, Afghanistan, in 2009. A 20-minute AMRAP of deadlifts, muscle-ups, and squat cleans.

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  9 Deadlifts 245lb
  8 Muscle Ups
  9 Squat Cleans 155lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 245-lb deadlifts, 155-lb squat cleans |
| Women | 165-lb deadlifts, 105-lb squat cleans |

## Profile
Marine 1st Lt. Michael E. Johnson, 25, of the 7th Communications Battalion, 3rd Marine Headquarters Group, III Marine Expeditionary Force, headquartered in Okinawa, Japan, died on September 8, 2009, while supporting combat operations in Kunar Province, Afghanistan. He is survived by his wife, Durinda. Johnson was first posted on crossfit.com on July 9, 2010.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Heavy pull and gymnastics couplet
- **Time Domain:** Fixed, 20-minute AMRAP
- **Intensity:** High — heavy deadlifts and cleans tax the posterior chain between muscle-up sets

## Collectible Metrics
- **Reps:** Total rounds and reps completed
