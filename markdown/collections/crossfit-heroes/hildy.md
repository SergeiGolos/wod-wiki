# Hildy

## Description
Honors Army combat camera specialist Spc. Hilda Clayton. A chipper for time, performed in a weight vest: 100-calorie rows sandwiching 75 thrusters, 50 pull-ups, and 75 wall-ball shots.

```time
20lb Weight Vest
100 Cal Row
75 Thrusters 45lb
50 Pullups
75 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
100 Cal Row
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 45-lb barbell, 20-lb medicine ball to a 10-foot target, 20-lb weight vest |
| Women | 35-lb barbell, 14-lb medicine ball to a 9-foot target, 14-lb weight vest |

## Profile
Army Spc. Hilda Clayton, 22, of Augusta, Georgia, died from injuries sustained when a mortar malfunctioned during an Afghan National Army training exercise in Qaraghahi, Afghanistan. Assigned to the 55th Signal Company and the 21st Signal Brigade at Fort Meade, Maryland, she was providing Combat Camera support at the time of her death. She is survived by her husband, Chase Clayton. First posted on crossfit.com on May 29, 2016.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Monostructural, weightlifting, and gymnastics
- **Time Domain:** Long — high-volume chipper
- **Primary Movements:** Rowing, thrusters, pull-ups, wall-ball shots
- **Intensity:** Very high — the vest multiplies every element
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate (vest work and volume)

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
