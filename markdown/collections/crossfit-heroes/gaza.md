# Gaza

## Profile

Air Force Capt. Lucas "Gaza" Gruenther, 32, chief of flight safety for the 31st Fighter Wing at Aviano Air Base, Italy, died January 28, 2013, when his F-16 went down in the Adriatic Sea. Five rounds of kettlebell swings, push-ups, pull-ups, box jumps, and a mile run.

```time
(5)
  35 Kettlebell Swings 53lb
  30 Pushups
  25 Pullups
  20 Box Jumps 30in
  1-mile Run
```
