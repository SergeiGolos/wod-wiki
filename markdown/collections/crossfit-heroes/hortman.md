# Hortman

```time
45:00 AMRAP
  800m Run
  80 Squats
  8 Muscle Ups
```

## Profile

U.S. Army Capt. John D. Hortman, 30, of Inman, South Carolina, assigned to the 1st Battalion, 160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment, based in Fort Campbell, Kentucky, died on Aug. 8, 2011, in Fort Benning, Georgia, in a helicopter accident during a military training exercise. He is survived by his mother, Brenda Jones; sister, Jill; and brother, Andy Pierce.

## Breakdown

- **Format:** As many rounds as possible in 45 minutes of an 800-meter run, 80 squats, and 8 muscle-ups.
- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Long, 45-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Run, air squat, muscle-up
- **Intensity:** Moderate to high
- **Skill Level:** Advanced

## Collectible Metrics

- **Reps:** Total rounds completed
