# Dallas

## Description
The "Dallas 5" workout honors the five officers killed in the July 7, 2016, Dallas ambush. Five 5-minute stations of maximum reps — burpees, deadlifts and box jumps, Turkish get-ups, snatches and push-ups, and calorie rowing — with 1 minute of rest between stations.

```time
5:00 ? Burpees
1:00 Rest
5:00 AMRAP
  7 Deadlifts 155lb
  7 Box Jumps 24in
1:00 Rest
5:00 ? Turkish Get-ups 40lb
1:00 Rest
5:00 AMRAP
  7 Snatches 75lb
  7 Pushups
1:00 Rest
5:00 ? Cal Row
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 155-lb deadlift, 24-inch box, 40-lb dumbbell, 75-lb snatch |
| Women | 105-lb deadlift, 20-inch box, 30-lb dumbbell, 55-lb snatch |

## Profile
Honors the five officers killed in the line of duty on July 7, 2016, when a gunman ambushed police working a peaceful protest in downtown Dallas: Dallas Police Senior Cpl. Lorne Ahrens, Sgt. Michael Smith, Officer Michael Krol, and Officer Patrick Zamarripa, and Dallas Area Rapid Transit Officer Brent Thompson. First posted on crossfit.com on July 8, 2017.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Mixed — gymnastics, weightlifting, and monostructural
- **Time Domain:** Fixed — five 5-minute stations, 29 minutes total
- **Primary Movements:** Burpees, deadlifts, box jumps, Turkish get-ups, snatches, push-ups, rowing
- **Intensity:** High — repeat max-effort efforts across stations

## Collectible Metrics
- **Reps:** Reps completed at each of the five stations
