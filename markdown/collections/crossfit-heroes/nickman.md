# Nickman

## Description
Honors Army Spc. Nicholas B. Burley. Ten rounds for time of a 200-meter farmers carry, 10 weighted pull-ups, and 20 alternating dumbbell power snatches.

```time
(10)
  200m Farmers Carry 55lb
  10 Weighted Pullups 35lb
  20 Alternating Dumbbell Power Snatches 55lb
```

## Scaling

| Sex | Loads |
|-----|-------|
| Men | 55-lb dumbbells for farmers carry and power snatches, 35-lb dumbbells for pull-ups |
| Women | 40-lb dumbbells for farmers carry and power snatches, 20-lb dumbbells for pull-ups |

## Profile
U.S. Army Spc. Nicholas B. Burley, 22, of Red Bluff, California, died July 30, 2013, in Logar Province, Afghanistan, of injuries caused by indirect fire. First posted on crossfit.com on September 3, 2016.

## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Strongman carry, gymnastics, and dumbbell weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Long — 10 rounds of grip-intensive work
- **Primary Movements:** Farmers carry, weighted pull-ups, alternating dumbbell power snatches
- **Intensity:** High — grip is the limiting factor
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (weighted pull-ups and dumbbell snatching)

## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** Total time to complete
