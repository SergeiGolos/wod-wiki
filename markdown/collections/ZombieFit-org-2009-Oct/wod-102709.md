# WOD 9

## Warm Up

```time
Run 1/2 mile | bike 1/2 mile | row 250m

(3)
  10 Parkour Squats/Air Squats
  20 Sit Ups
  10 Push Ups
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner

```time
(5)
  10 Jumping Pull Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Twelve Inch Box Jumps
```

### Avancée — Advanced

```time
(5)
  10 Pull Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Twenty-Four Inch Box Jumps
```

### Traceur — Expert

```time
(5)
  10 L-Sit Pull Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Thirty-Six Inch Box Jumps
```

## Cool Down

200m quadrupedal movement.
