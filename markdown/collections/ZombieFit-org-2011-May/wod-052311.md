# WOD 400

## Warm Up

```time
Run 1/4 mile | row 250m | bike 1/2 mile

(3)
  25m Quadrupedal Movement
  10 Push Ups
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Pull Ups | Jumping Pull Ups
```

```time
40 Jumping Jacks
20 Sit Ups
40 Jumping Jacks
20 Air Squats
40 Jumping Jacks
20 Jumping Pull Ups
1:00 Rest
400m Sprint
```
