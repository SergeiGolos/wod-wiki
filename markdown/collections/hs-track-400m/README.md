# HS Track 400m — Raffensperger

400m training for high school runners, transcribed from *400m Training*, John Raffensperger's Iowa Track & Field Coaching Clinic handout (freely published; Raffensperger is a decades-long Iowa high school coach known for 400m development). The catalog carries his three published sample season weeks day by day — Week 3 (early tempo), Week 7 (first week of April), Week 11 (week after Drake Relays, sharpening toward a :50.5 goal) — plus the phase menus, race-pace rules, lifting cycle, and 4×400 relay guidance in the [program overview](/collections/hs-track-400m/program-overview).

## Conventions
- One file per training day: `week-NN-<day>.md`, frontmatter `week`, `day`, `domain: track`, `intent: training`, `format: intervals`. Week numbers are the source's own (#3, #7, #11); meets and conditional flip days get no file.
- Target splits inline (`@ :45-:49`, `@ 1:08-1:14`); `*M:SS Rest` marks required rest; walk/jog recoveries are movement lines (`100m Walk`).
- Terse source lines are transcribed as written with the reading noted in `## Notes`; lifting and plyometrics stay in Notes, never in the `time` block.
