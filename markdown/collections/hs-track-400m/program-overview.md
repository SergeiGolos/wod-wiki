# Raffensperger 400m — Overview
**Source:** "400m Training," John Raffensperger — Iowa Track & Field Coaching Clinic handout (freely published PDF). Raffensperger is a decades-long Iowa high school coach known for his 400m development.

## Philosophy

- Hard/easy day alternation.
- Quality over quantity for speed.
- Don't peak too early in a 13-14 week season.
- The first 200 of the 400 = 200 PR + 2s (boy: 22.5 → 24.5; girl: 27.5 → 29.5-30.0).

### Season Phases

- **Early** — speed + max strength + learn tempo: 6-8×30m from 3/4-point stance, 6×flying 20s, 4×110 buildups, 4-5×150 med-fast-faster, 200+100+200 ×4-5 sets, 6×150, 8×50-80 pickups, 4×50 top-effort sets, hills/stadiums.
- **Mid** — tempo endurance: 8×200, 6×300, 50-100-150-200-250-300 with walk-equal-rest, 500-400-300-200 ladder + 2×150, med ball after; speed endurance: 6×150 with full recovery, 5×200, 4×300.
- **Late** — 200/200 pairs building to 800m total, 300+100 with walk-100 between, 5×250 @80-90%, 4×300 @80-90%, race-pace doubles.

### Race Pace Simulation

```time
(2)
  200m Run @ 25.5
  *1:00 Rest
  200m Run @ 25.5
  *5:00 Rest
```

- Worked example: goal 48.5 → run the 200s at 25.5. The rule: 25.5 = 200 PR + ~3s (each half of the race is the 200 PR plus ~3s).
- Pace-workout options: 4 sets of 3×100 @ :14-:15 (90s rep rest, 3min set rest); 4 sets of straight-curve-straight-curve @ :15-:17.

### Competitive Season (curve work)

- Block starts 3-4×50m (fast first 50).
- 3×flying 120 on the curve.
- 4×200 at race pace (jog 100 / walk 100 recovery).
- 2×(4×100 with 1min rest) with 8min between sets.
- If the meet is Friday, flip Friday/Saturday workouts.

### Strength Cycle

- Lifts: box squat, power clean, bench, deadlift.
- 4-week wave: 3×3 @80% → 5×5 → 5-4-3-2-1 climbing → 6-8-10 climbing.

### Off-Season Supports

- Warm-up: 10-20-40 buildups from a 2-point stance, walk-back recovery.
- Plyos: hops, bounds, squat jumps, czar jumps.
- Med ball throws.
- Sled pulls 2×25m, then 3×30m unresisted (15-25lb plates).
- Wickets / hurdle hops.

### 4x400 Relay

- Leg order rationale: lead-off is the tough lane runner; 2nd is the curve fighter through the 3-turn stagger; 3rd is the competitor who closes deficits; anchor is the fastest finisher.
- Attack the first 50m.
- Lift arms and knees at 200m.
- Stay relaxed the last 100.
