# WOD 14

## Warm Up

Run 1/4 mile | bike 2 miles | row 500m followed by:

```time
(3)
  10 Pull Ups
  10 Push Ups
  10 Parkour Squats
  10 Sit Ups
```

## WOD

```time
400m Forward Quadrupedal Movement
```

## Cool Down

```time
10 Precision Jumps at 85% of your max broad jump
```
