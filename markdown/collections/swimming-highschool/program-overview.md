# High School Swimming Program — Season & Championship Taper Overview

**Source & Methodology:** Modeled on the 12-week high school championship macrocycle (Podium Training Systems / Coach Dave Burgess; NISCA All-America training frameworks; Carmel High School championship structures). Tailored to short-course yards (SCY) high school competition.

## 1. Macrocycle Structure

The standard high school interscholastic season spans 12 weeks, split into two primary operational phases:

### Phase I: Standard Season (Weeks 1–9)
- **Weeks 1–3: Aerobic Foundation & Stroke Mechanics.** Base volume (4,500–6,000 yd/session), aerobic threshold (EN1/EN2), kicking endurance, and early-season drill mechanics.
- **Weeks 4–6: Threshold Progression & Dual Meet Rhythm.** Weekly Tuesday/Thursday dual-meet cadence, lactate threshold sets, descending interval work, and underwater dolphin kick volume.
- **Weeks 7–9: Race Pace & Speed Endurance.** Anaerobic capacity (SP1/SP2), broken 100/200 race simulations, mid-season invitational prep, and relay transition training.

### Phase II: Competition Training & Championship Taper (Weeks 10–12)
The progressive 3-week taper leading into Conference, Sectional, and State/National Championship meets.
- **Week 10 (Taper Phase 1 — Volume Drop):** Volume reduced to 70–75% (~3,800 yd). Intensity maintained; focus on power, short burst speed, and neuromuscular recruitment.
- **Week 11 (Taper Phase 2 — Speed Sharpening):** Volume reduced to 50–60% (~3,000 yd). Conference/Sectional meet prep; broken swims at target race splits; extended rest intervals.
- **Week 12 (Taper Phase 3 — Peak Race Activation):** Volume reduced to 35–45% (~2,000–2,400 yd). State Championship week; start reaction times, relay takeoffs, turns/breakout speed, and central nervous system recovery.

## 2. Weekly Training Rhythm (In-Season vs Championship)

| Day | Standard Season (Weeks 1–9) | Championship Taper (Weeks 10–12) |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Aerobic Capacity + Threshold (5,500 yd) | Speed & Neuromuscular Sharpening (3,500 yd) |
| **Tuesday** | Dual Meet / Race Simulation | Active Recovery & Technique Touch (2,500 yd) |
| **Wednesday** | Recovery Aerobic + IM Stroke Work (4,500 yd) | Race Pace Broken 50s/100s + Rest (2,800 yd) |
| **Thursday** | Dual Meet / Anaerobic Power (4,800 yd) | Starts, Turns & Relay Exchanges (2,200 yd) |
| **Friday** | Pre-Meet Tune-Up / Sprint Sets (4,000 yd) | Activation / Pre-Race Primer (1,800 yd) |
| **Saturday** | Invitational Meet or Long Aerobic Set | **Championship Meet (State/Nationals)** |
| **Sunday** | Rest | Rest |
