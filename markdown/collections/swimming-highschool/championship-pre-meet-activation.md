# Championship Pre-Meet Activation

**Duration**: 45 minutes  
**Phase**: Competition Training (Championship Taper — Week 12 State/National Meet Week)  
**Total Volume**: ~1,800 yards  

### Description
The final pre-meet activation session executed 24 to 48 hours prior to state or national championship competition. Training volume drops below 2,000 yards. The sole focus is feel for the water, explosive start reaction, streamline breakout velocity, and relay exchange precision without accumulating physical or neural fatigue.

```time
(1) Warmup
  400yd Freestyle
  (4)
    50yd IM Drill
    :15 Rest

(4) Dive Sprints
  25yd Sprint Freestyle
  1:30 Rest

(4) Relay Exchanges
  50yd Race Pace Freestyle
  2:00 Rest

(1) Cooldown
  300yd Easy Swim
```

### Breakdown
- **Warmup**: 600yd loose loosening and IM drills to awaken neuromuscular feel
- **Dive Sprints**: 100yd start reaction and 15m breakout bursts off the starting blocks
- **Relay Exchanges**: 200yd timed relay starts and turnover at championship pace
- **Cooldown**: 300yd relaxed flush swim

### Collectible Metrics
- Reaction time and breakout velocity
- Relay exchange differential times
