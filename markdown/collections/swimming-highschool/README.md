# Swimming: High School

Short-course yards (SCY) high school interscholastic swimming programs structured across the standard dual-meet season and peak championship competition training (state & national taper).

## Overview
- [Program Overview](/collections/swimming-highschool/program-overview) — 12-week macrocycle structure, energy systems, volume progression, and taper physiology.

## Standard Season (In-Season & Dual Meets)
High-volume aerobic base, lactate threshold, stroke mechanics, and mid-season race preparation (Weeks 1–9):
- [Distance Freestyle Training](/collections/swimming-highschool/distance-freestyle-training) — Aerobic endurance and negative-split pacing (90 min)
- [IM Training Session](/collections/swimming-highschool/im-training-session) — Four-stroke versatility and transition turns (90 min)
- [Sprint Freestyle Power](/collections/swimming-highschool/sprint-freestyle-power) — High-velocity sprint mechanics and turnover (75 min)
- [Race Simulation Day](/collections/swimming-highschool/race-simulation-day) — Broken race pace and dual-meet rehearsal (60 min)

## Competition Training (Championship Taper)
Low-volume, high-velocity sharpening for Conference, State, and National Championship meets (Weeks 10–12):
- [Taper Sprint Sharpening](/collections/swimming-highschool/taper-sprint-sharpening) — Weeks 10–11 speed retention and ATP-CP alactic power
- [Championship Pre-Meet Activation](/collections/swimming-highschool/championship-pre-meet-activation) — Week 12 state-meet warmup, block starts, and relay exchanges
