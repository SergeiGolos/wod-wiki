# WOD 174

## Warm Up

```time
Run 1/4 mile | bike 1/2 mile | row 250m

(3)
  10 Alternating Leg Lunges
  10 Sit Ups
  10 Push Ups
  10 Air Squats
```

## WOD

### Étudiant — Beginner

Complete as many rounds as possible in 10 minutes of:

```time
10:00 AMRAP
  5 Five-Foot Precisions
  50m Quadrupedal Movement
  10 Push Ups
```

### Avancée — Advanced

Complete as many rounds as possible in 15 minutes of:

```time
15:00 AMRAP
  7 Seven-Foot Precisions
  50m Quadrupedal Movement
  10 Push Ups
```

### Traceur — Expert

Complete as many rounds as possible in 20 minutes of:

```time
20:00 AMRAP
  7 Seven-Foot Precisions
  50m Quadrupedal Movement
  10 Push Ups
```

## Cool Down

25 pull ups (kipping, dead hang | jumping)
