# HS Track Sprints — Arrowhead

Sprint training for high school 100–400m runners, transcribed from *Sprint Training: Off Season to Peak Season* — the clinic deck by Chris Herriot, 17-year head track coach at Arrowhead High School (WI) and past WISTCA president (publicly distributed clinic material). 27 training days across two January rotations of the same weekly template: Monday acceleration, Wednesday hills, Friday speed — every set, rest, and intensity as prescribed. The in-season template week lives in the [program overview](/collections/hs-track-sprints/program-overview).

## Conventions
- One file per training day: `week-NN-<day>.md`, frontmatter `week`, `day`, `domain: track`, `intent: training`, `format: intervals`.
- Intensities inline (`@ 90%`, `@ 95%`); `*M:SS Rest` marks required rest after the rep it follows; recoveries are movement lines (`50m Walk`).
- Sets-of-sets are fully expanded under a `(N)` sets header; lifting, drills, and plyos are summarized in `## Notes`, never in the `time` block.
