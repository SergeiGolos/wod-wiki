# 2012 CrossFit Games - Event 9 "Rope-Sled"

**Location:** Home Depot Center Track, Carson, California
**Date:** July 14, 2012 (Day 3 - Saturday)

## Description

Starting immediately after the Sprint, five rounds of a 20-foot rope climb and a 20-yard padded sled drive down the field, 100 yards in total.

```time
(5)
  20ft Rope Climb
  20yd Sled Drive
```

## Scaling

| Level | Sled |
|-------|------|
| Men | Padded sled with 90 lb added |
| Women | Unweighted padded sled |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 13-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Rope climb, sled drive
- **Intensity:** Very high
- **Skill Level:** Advanced

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
