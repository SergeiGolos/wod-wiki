# 2011 CrossFit Games - Event 3 "Rope-Clean"

**Location:** Home Depot Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 29, 2011 (Day 1 - Friday)

## Description

Descending 15-foot rope climbs paired with clean and jerks that climb in load each round, from 5 ascents and 5 reps at 145 lb down to a single 225-lb clean and jerk; won by Rich Froning a year after rope climbs cost him the title.

```time
5 Rope Climbs
5 Clean & Jerks 145lb
4 Rope Climbs
4 Clean & Jerks 165lb
3 Rope Climbs
3 Clean & Jerks 185lb
2 Rope Climbs
2 Clean & Jerks 205lb
1 Rope Climb
1 Clean & Jerk 225lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Clean & Jerk |
|-------|--------------|
| Men | 145-225 lb (5→1 reps) |
| Women | 115-155 lb (5→1 reps) |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, no time cap
- **Primary Movements:** Rope climb, clean and jerk
- **Intensity:** High
- **Skill Level:** Advanced

## Collectible Metrics
