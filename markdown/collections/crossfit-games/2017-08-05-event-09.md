# 2017 CrossFit Games - Event 9 "Muscle-Up Clean Ladder"

**Location:** Alliant Energy Center, Madison, Wisconsin
**Date:** August 5, 2017 (Day 3 - Saturday)

## Description

8 rounds for time: 4 bar muscle-ups, then 2 cleans at an ascending barbell. Athletes unable to finish are ranked by total reps, ties broken by time of last successful rep.

```time
(8)
4 Bar Muscle Ups
2 Cleans
```

## Scaling

| Level | Cleans |
|-------|--------|
| Men | 225-350 lb across 8 bars |
| Women | 145-235 lb across 8 bars |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 11-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Bar muscle-up, clean
- **Intensity:** Very high
- **Skill Level:** Advanced

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
