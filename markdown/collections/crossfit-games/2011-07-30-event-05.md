# 2011 CrossFit Games - Event 5 "Skills 2"

**Location:** Home Depot Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 30, 2011 (Day 2 - Saturday)

## Description

Three ranked tests summed by placement: a 1-rep-max weighted chest-to-bar pull-up in two minutes, a 1-rep-max snatch in two minutes, and a 60-second carry of two weighted water jugs for distance.

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Short, fixed 2-minute, 2-minute, and 60-second test windows
- **Primary Movements:** Weighted chest-to-bar pull-up, snatch, jug carry
- **Intensity:** Maximal effort

## Collectible Metrics

- **Weight:** Heaviest weighted chest-to-bar pull-up
- **Weight:** Heaviest snatch
- **Distance:** Jug carry distance in 60 seconds
