# 2010 CrossFit Games - Event 6 "Cleans-Handstand Push-ups"

**Location:** Home Depot Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 18, 2010 (Day 3 - Sunday)

## Description

Seven rounds of three heavy cleans and four handstand push-ups, done on rings for the men and on the floor for the women, against a 12-minute cap.

```time
(7)
  3 Cleans 205lb
  4 Ring Handstand Pushups
```

## Scaling

| Level | Cleans | Handstand Push-ups |
|-------|--------|--------------------|
| Men | 205 lb | Ring handstand push-ups |
| Women | 135 lb | Floor handstand push-ups |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 12-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Clean, handstand push-up
- **Intensity:** Very high
- **Skill Level:** Advanced

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
