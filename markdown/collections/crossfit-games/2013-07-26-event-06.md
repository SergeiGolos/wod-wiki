# 2013 CrossFit Games - Event 6 "Legless"

**Location:** StubHub Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 26, 2013 (Day 2 - Friday)

## Description

Alternating sets of thrusters and no-legs rope climbs, descending each round: 27/4, 21/3, 15/2, 9/1. Rope climbs are 15 feet.

```time
27 Thrusters 95lb
4 Legless Rope Climbs
21 Thrusters 95lb
3 Legless Rope Climbs
15 Thrusters 95lb
2 Legless Rope Climbs
9 Thrusters 95lb
1 Legless Rope Climb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Thrusters |
|-------|-----------|
| Men | 95 lb |
| Women | 65 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 10-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Thruster, legless rope climb
- **Intensity:** Very high
