# 2009 CrossFit Games - Event 8 "Chipper"

**Location:** The Ranch, Aromas, California
**Date:** July 12, 2009 (Day 3 - Sunday)

## Description

The final chipper: ten movements from cleans through overhead walking lunges, completing each exercise's repetitions before moving to the next.

```time
15 Cleans 155lb
30 Toes to Bars
30 Box Jumps 24in
15 Muscle Ups
30 Dumbbell Push Presses 40lb
30 Double Unders
15 Thrusters 135lb
30 Pullups
30 Burpees
300ft Overhead Walking Lunges 45lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Cleans | Box Jumps | Dumbbell Push Presses | Thrusters | Overhead Walking Lunges |
|-------|--------|-----------|----------------------|-----------|-------------------------|
| Men | 155 lb | 24 in | 40 lb | 135 lb | 45 lb plate |
| Women | 100 lb | 20 in | 25 lb | 95 lb | 25 lb plate |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Long, no published time cap
- **Primary Movements:** Cleans, toes to bar, box jumps, muscle-ups, dumbbell push presses, double-unders, thrusters, pull-ups, burpees, overhead lunges
- **Intensity:** Very high
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (muscle-ups, double-unders)

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
