# 2009 CrossFit Games - Event 4 "Row / Hammer Stake"

**Location:** The Ranch, Aromas, California
**Date:** July 10, 2009 (Day 1 - Friday)

## Description

Two 500-meter Concept-2 rows separated by pounding a stake into the ground — 4-foot stake for men, 3-foot for women.

```time
500m Row
1 Stake Pound
500m Row
```

## Scaling

| Level | Stake |
|-------|-------|
| Men | 4 ft |
| Women | 3 ft |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Short, no published time cap
- **Primary Movements:** Rowing, sledgehammer striking
- **Intensity:** High
