# 2010 CrossFit Games - Event 5 "Sandbag Move"

**Location:** Home Depot Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 17, 2010 (Day 2 - Saturday)

## Description

An odd-object stadium trek: move a total sandbag load down the stairs, across the stadium floor, up and over the wall, and up the stairs at the far end.

```time
600lb Sandbag Move
```

## Scaling

| Level | Sandbags |
|-------|----------|
| Men | 600 lb total |
| Women | 370 lb total |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Strongman
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, for-time odd-object move
- **Primary Movements:** Sandbag carry, stair climb, wall traverse
- **Intensity:** High

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
