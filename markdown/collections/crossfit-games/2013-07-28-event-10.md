# 2013 CrossFit Games - Event 10 "Sprint Chipper"

**Location:** StubHub Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 28, 2013 (Day 4 - Sunday)

## Description

Medicine-ball GHD sit-ups, snatches, and burpees over a 6-foot wall. The top 30 men and 30 women advanced to the final heat.

```time
21 Med Ball GHD Situps
15 Snatches 165lb
9 Wall Burpees
```
