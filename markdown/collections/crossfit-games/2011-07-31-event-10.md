# 2011 CrossFit Games - Event 10 "The End 3"

**Location:** Home Depot Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 31, 2011 (Day 3 - Sunday)

## Description

The final event of the Games: the same eight-station chipper raced for time two minutes after The End 2, contested by the 12 remaining men and 12 remaining women.

```time
20cal Row
30 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
20 Toes to Bar
30 Box Jumps 24in
20 Sumo Deadlift High Pulls 108lb
30 Burpees
20 Shoulder to Overhead 135lb
Sled Pull
```

## Scaling

| Level | Wall Ball | KB Sumo Deadlift High Pull | Shoulder to Overhead |
|-------|-----------|----------------------------|----------------------|
| Men | 20 lb | 108 lb KB | 135 lb |
| Women | 14 lb | 72 lb KB | 95 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, no time cap
- **Primary Movements:** Row, wall ball, toes-to-bar, box jump, sumo deadlift high pull, burpee, shoulder-to-overhead, sled pull
- **Intensity:** Sprint

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
