# 2011 CrossFit Games - Event 7 "Dog-Sled"

**Location:** Home Depot Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 31, 2011 (Day 3 - Sunday)

## Description

Three rounds of double-unders and 135-lb overhead squats, then three rounds of handstand push-ups finishing with a 40-foot push of a sled weighted to 385 lb; Annie Thorisdottir took the women's win.

```time
(3)
  30 Double Unders
  10 Overhead Squats 135lb

(3)
  10 Handstand Pushups
  40ft Sled Push 385lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Overhead Squat | Sled Push |
|-------|----------------|-----------|
| Men | 135 lb | 385 lb sled |
| Women | 95 lb | 275 lb sled |
