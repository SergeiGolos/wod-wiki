# 2009 CrossFit Games - Event 5 "Couplet"

**Location:** The Ranch, Aromas, California
**Date:** July 10, 2009 (Day 1 - Friday)

## Description

Three rounds of 30 wall-ball shots and 30 squat snatches, closing out the first day for the survivors of the cuts.

```time
(3)
  30 Wall Ball Shots 20lb
  30 Squat Snatches 75lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Wall Ball Shots | Squat Snatches |
|-------|-----------------|----------------|
| Men | 20 lb ball | 75 lb |
| Women | 14 lb ball | 45 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal, gymnastic and weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, no published time cap
- **Primary Movements:** Wall ball shots, squat snatches
- **Intensity:** Very high
- **Skill Level:** Advanced (full-depth squat snatch at volume)

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
