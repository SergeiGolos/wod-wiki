# 2008 CrossFit Games - Event 2 "Deadlift Burpees"

**Location:** The Ranch, Aromas, California
**Date:** July 5, 2008 (Day 1 - Saturday)

## Description

Workout B paired heavy deadlifts with burpees, 5 rounds of 5 deadlifts and 10 burpees under a 12-minute limit.

```time
(5)
5 Deadlifts 275lb
10 Burpees
```
