# 2015 CrossFit Games - Event 4 "Snatch Speed Ladder"

**Location:** StubHub Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 24, 2015 (Day 2 - Friday)

## Description

Race through progressively heavier squat snatches across three rounds. Quarterfinal: 5 bars, top 20 advance. Semifinal: 5 heavier bars, top 10 advance. Final: 5 heavier bars. Ranked by time through each ladder or heaviest weight snatched.

```time
1 Squat Snatch ?lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Ladder |
|-------|--------|
| Men | 190-275 lb across 15 bars |
| Women | 110-180 lb across 15 bars |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting
- **Primary Movement:** Squat snatch
- **Intensity:** Maximum effort

## Collectible Metrics

- **Weight:** Heaviest snatch completed
