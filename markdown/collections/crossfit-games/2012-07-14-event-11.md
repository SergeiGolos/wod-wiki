# 2012 CrossFit Games - Event 11 "Chipper"

**Location:** Home Depot Center Stadium, Carson, California
**Date:** July 14, 2012 (Day 3 - Saturday)

## Description

A 110-repetition chipper up and back down a ladder of overhead squats, box jump overs, fat-bar thrusters, power cleans, toes-to-bar, and burpee muscle-ups; the field was cut to 24 after this event.

```time
10 Overhead Squats 155lb
10 Box Jump Overs 24in
10 Fat Bar Thrusters 135lb
10 Power Cleans 205lb
10 Toes To Bar
10 Burpee Muscle Ups
10 Toes To Bar
10 Power Cleans 205lb
10 Fat Bar Thrusters 135lb
10 Box Jump Overs 24in
10 Overhead Squats 155lb
```
