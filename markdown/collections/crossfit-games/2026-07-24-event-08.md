# 2026 CrossFit Games - Event 8 "Climbing Snail '26"

**Location:** SAP Center, San Jose, California
**Date:** July 24, 2026 (Day 2 - Friday)

## Description

Heavy Snail pushes bracket a gymnastics couplet of legless rope climbs and toes-to-bar; women push the 400-lb Snail 60 feet wearing a 16-lb vest and climb 2 legless plus 4 rope climbs, while men push 100 feet in a 22-lb vest and climb 3 legless plus 5.
