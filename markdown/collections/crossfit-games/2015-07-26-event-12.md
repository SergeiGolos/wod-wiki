# 2015 CrossFit Games - Event 12 "Pedal to the Metal 1"

**Location:** StubHub Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 26, 2015 (Day 4 - Sunday)

## Description

Pegboard ascents, row, Assault Bike, and alternating-arm dumbbell squat snatches. The pegboard debuted here and broke most of the field — only three women finished the ascents. Pedal to the Metal 2 began two minutes later.

```time
3 Pegboard Ascents
24cal Row
16cal Assault Bike
8 Dumbbell Squat Snatches 100lb
```
