# 2016 CrossFit Games - Event 9 "The Separator"

**Location:** StubHub Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 23, 2016 (Day 4 - Saturday)

## Description

Ring handstand push-ups and squat ladders with burpees between sets. Men start with handstand push-ups; women start with back squats. The event was performed in a different order for the men and women, with reduced scoring for athletes failing the minimum work requirement.

```time
12 Ring Handstand Pushups
15 Back Squats 225lb
20 Burpees
9 Ring Handstand Pushups
18 Front Squats 205lb
20 Burpees
6 Ring Handstand Pushups
21 Overhead Squats 185lb
20 Burpees
```

## Scaling

| Level | Ring HSPU | Squats |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| Men | 12-9-6 | Back 225 lb, Front 205 lb, OHS 185 lb |
| Women | 6-4-2 | Back 165 lb, Front 145 lb, OHS 125 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 16-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Ring handstand push-up, back squat, front squat, overhead squat, burpee
- **Intensity:** Very high
- **Skill Level:** Advanced

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
