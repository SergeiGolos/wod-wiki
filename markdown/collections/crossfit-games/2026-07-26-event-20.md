# 2026 CrossFit Games - Event 20 "Fibonacci Final"

**Location:** SAP Center, San Jose, California
**Date:** July 26, 2026 (Day 4 - Sunday)

## Description

The men's final follows Fibonacci numbers: 5-8-13 reps of deficit handstand push-ups and double-kettlebell deadlifts, then a 89-foot (about 27 m) two-kettlebell overhead walking lunge to close the Games.

```time
(5-8-13)
```
