# 2011 CrossFit Games - Event 6 "Killer Kage"

**Location:** Home Depot Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 30, 2011 (Day 2 - Saturday)

## Description

Three rounds of seven 225-lb front squats, a 700-meter bike, and a 100-foot monkey bar traverse.

(3)

  700m Bike
  100ft Monkey Bar Traverse
