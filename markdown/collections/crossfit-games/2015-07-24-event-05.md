# 2015 CrossFit Games - Event 5 "Heavy DT"

**Location:** StubHub Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 24, 2015 (Day 2 - Friday)

## Description

5 rounds for time of the barbell complex from the Hero workout "DT" at 205/145 lb. The community voted between Double DT and Heavy DT; Heavy DT won.

```time
(5)
12 Deadlifts 205lb
9 Hang Power Cleans 205lb
6 Push Jerks 205lb
```

| Level | Barbell |

| Men | 205 lb |
| Women | 145 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 12-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Deadlift, hang power clean, push jerk
- **Intensity:** Very high
