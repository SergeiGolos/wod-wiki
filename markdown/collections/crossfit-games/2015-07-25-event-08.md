# 2015 CrossFit Games - Event 8 "Soccer Chipper"

**Location:** StubHub Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 25, 2015 (Day 3 - Saturday)

## Description

Flip the 560-lb Pig 100 feet to the rig, climb 4 legless ropes, then handstand walk 100 feet in 50-foot unbroken sections — coming down meant restarting that section.

```time
100ft Pig Flip 560lb
4 Legless Rope Climbs
100ft Handstand Walk
```

| Men | 560 lb |
| Women | 395 lb |
