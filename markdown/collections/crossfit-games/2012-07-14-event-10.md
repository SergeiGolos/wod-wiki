# 2012 CrossFit Games - Event 10 "Clean Ladder"

**Location:** Home Depot Center Stadium, Carson, California
**Date:** July 14, 2012 (Day 3 - Saturday)

## Description

A clean ladder with one successful lift every 30 seconds on progressively heavier barbells, ranked by the heaviest successful clean, with partial-pound credit for additional deadlifts after a failed attempt; the field was cut to 36 after this event.

```time
1 Clean ?lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Clean |
|-------|-------|
| Men | Start 245 lb, +10 lb every 30 s, to 385 lb |
| Women | Start 140 lb, +10 lb every 30 s to 195 lb, then +5 lb to 235 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 30 seconds per barbell
- **Primary Movements:** Clean
- **Intensity:** Maximum effort

## Collectible Metrics

- **Weight:** Heaviest successful clean
