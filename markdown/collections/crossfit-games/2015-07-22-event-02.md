# 2015 CrossFit Games - Event 2 "Sandbag 2015"

**Location:** StubHub Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 22, 2015 (Day 1 - Wednesday)

## Description

Move all the sandbags from the top of the stadium stairs down to the floor, across the stadium by wheelbarrow, over a wall, and up the stairs on the opposite side.

```time
Sandbag Move 720lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Sandbags |
|-------|----------|
| Men | 4 x 100 lb + 4 x 80 lb |
| Women | 4 x 70 lb + 4 x 50 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 15-minute cap
- **Primary Movement:** Sandbag carry
- **Intensity:** Very high

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
