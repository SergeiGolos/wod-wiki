# 2016 CrossFit Games - Event 2 "Ranch Deadlift Ladder"

**Location:** The Ranch, Aromas, California
**Date:** July 20, 2016 (Day 1 - Wednesday)

## Description

One deadlift every 30 seconds through 20 progressively heavier barbells, starting 10 minutes after the trail run. Athletes lift in reverse order of their run finish.

```time
1 Deadlift ?lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Ladder |
|-------|--------|
| Men | 425-615 lb across 20 bars |
| Women | 275-415 lb across 20 bars |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting
- **Primary Movement:** Deadlift
- **Intensity:** Maximum effort

## Collectible Metrics

- **Weight:** Heaviest deadlift completed
