# 2010 CrossFit Games - Event 7 "The Final 1"

**Location:** Home Depot Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 18, 2010 (Day 3 - Sunday)

## Description

First leg of the three-part Final: three rounds of thirty hand-release push-ups, a climb over the stadium wall, and twenty-one overhead squats against a 7-minute cap.

```time
(3)
  30 Hand Release Pushups
  Over the Wall
  21 Overhead Squats 95lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Overhead Squats |
|-------|-----------------|
| Men | 95 lb |
| Women | 65 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Short, 7-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Hand-release push-up, wall traverse, overhead squat
- **Intensity:** Very high

## Collectible Metrics
