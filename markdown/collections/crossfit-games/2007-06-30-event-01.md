# 2007 CrossFit Games - Event 1 "Hopper WOD"

**Location:** The Ranch, Aromas, California
**Date:** June 30, 2007 (Day 1 - Saturday)

## Description

The workout drawn at random from the hopper on the morning of the first Games: a 1,000-meter row, then 5 rounds of 25 pull-ups and 7 push jerks.
