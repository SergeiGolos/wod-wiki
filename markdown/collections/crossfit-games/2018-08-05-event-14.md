# 2018 CrossFit Games - Event 14 "Aeneas"

**Location:** Alliant Energy Center, Madison, Wisconsin
**Date:** August 5, 2018 (Day 4 - Sunday)

## Description

Chipper: pegboard ascents, 40 thrusters, then three 33-foot yoke carries with the weight increased between each carry.

```time
5 Pegboard Ascents
40 Thrusters 85lb
33ft Yoke Carry 425lb
33ft Yoke Carry 565lb
33ft Yoke Carry 665lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Pegboards | Thrusters | Yoke |
|-------|-----------|-----------|------|
| Men | 5 | 85 lb | 425-565-665 lb |
| Women | 4 | 55 lb | 345-405-445 lb |
