# 2013 CrossFit Games - Event 8 "Clean & Jerk Ladder"

**Location:** StubHub Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 27, 2013 (Day 3 - Saturday)

## Description

One clean and jerk every 90 seconds with progressively heavier barbells, from one of four starting points. Athletes missing their opening weight are eliminated; after the first successful clean and jerk, a successful clean with a missed jerk earns partial credit.

```time
1 Clean & Jerk ?lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Ladder |
|-------|--------|
| Men | 225-405 lb across 19 bars |
| Women | 145-250 lb across 22 bars |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting
- **Primary Movement:** Clean and jerk
- **Intensity:** Maximum effort

## Collectible Metrics

- **Weight:** Heaviest successful clean and jerk
