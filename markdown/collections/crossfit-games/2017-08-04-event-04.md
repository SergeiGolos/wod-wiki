# 2017 CrossFit Games - Event 4 "Sprint O-Course"

**Location:** Alliant Energy Center, Madison, Wisconsin
**Date:** August 4, 2017 (Day 2 - Friday)

## Description

Head-to-head obstacle-course racing in a 3-round bracket: cargo net climb, wall climb, monkey bars, log balances, and a rope ladder. Heats of 5, with places 1-5 awarded from the final and remaining places by round times.

```time
Sprint Obstacle Course
```

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Sprint
- **Primary Movements:** Cargo net climb, wall climb, monkey bars, log balance, rope ladder
- **Intensity:** Maximum effort

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete the course
