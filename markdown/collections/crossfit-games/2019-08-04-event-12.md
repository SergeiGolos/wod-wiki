# 2019 CrossFit Games - Event 12 "The Standard"

**Location:** Alliant Energy Center, Madison, Wisconsin
**Date:** August 4, 2019 (Day 4 - Sunday)

## Description

Three benchmark workouts back to back: "Grace," muscle-ups, then "Isabel."

```time
30 Clean & Jerks 135lb
30 Muscle Ups
30 Snatches 135lb
```
