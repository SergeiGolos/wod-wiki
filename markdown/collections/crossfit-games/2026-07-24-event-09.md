# 2026 CrossFit Games - Event 9 "3D Throw"

**Location:** SAP Center, San Jose, California
**Date:** July 24, 2026 (Day 2 - Friday)

## Description

Three maximal throws of a heavy ball — one to the right, one to the left, and one backward — scored on combined distance.

```time
1 Throw Right 30lb
1 Throw Left 30lb
1 Throw Backward 30lb
```

|-------|------|
| Men | 30 lb |
| Women | 20 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Explosive power
- **Time Domain:** Short, no cap

- **Intensity:** Maximal effort

## Collectible Metrics
