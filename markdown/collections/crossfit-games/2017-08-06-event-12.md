# 2017 CrossFit Games - Event 12 "2223 Intervals"

**Location:** Alliant Energy Center, Madison, Wisconsin
**Date:** August 6, 2017 (Day 4 - Sunday)

## Description

2-minute intervals with 1 minute of rest: 2 rope climbs and 10/7 SkiErg calories, then as many overhead squats as possible in the remaining time. Repeat until 75 overhead squats are complete; the fourth interval is 3 minutes. Score is total time, with capped athletes ranked by OHS reps.

```time
(3) 2:00
2 Rope Climbs
10cal SkiErg
? Overhead Squats 155lb
1:00 Rest
3:00
2 Rope Climbs
10cal SkiErg
? Overhead Squats 155lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | SkiErg | Overhead Squats |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| Men | 10 cal | 155 lb |
| Women | 7 cal | 105 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 12-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Rope climb, SkiErg, overhead squat
- **Intensity:** Very high
- **Skill Level:** Advanced

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to reach 75 overhead squats
