# 2016 CrossFit Games - Event 6 "Squat Clean Pyramid"

**Location:** StubHub Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 22, 2016 (Day 3 - Friday)

## Description

Squat cleans at five stations of decreasing reps and increasing weights, each with a cut-off time: 10 reps by 2:00, 8 by 4:00, 6 by 6:00, 4 by 8:00, and 2 by 11:00. Athletes who miss a cut-off are ranked by reps completed at their last full station.

```time
10 Squat Cleans 245lb
8 Squat Cleans 265lb
6 Squat Cleans 285lb
4 Squat Cleans 305lb
2 Squat Cleans 325lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Squat Cleans |
|-------|--------------|

| Women | 165-180-195-205-215 lb |
