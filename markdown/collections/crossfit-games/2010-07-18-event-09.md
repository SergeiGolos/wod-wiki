# 2010 CrossFit Games - Event 9 "The Final 3"

**Location:** Home Depot Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 18, 2010 (Day 3 - Sunday)

## Description

The deciding leg of the three-part Final, where Rich Froning's legless rope climbs handed the title to Graham Holmberg: three rounds of five burpee wall jumps and 20-foot rope climbs against a 12-minute cap.

```time
(3)
  5 Burpee Wall Jumps
  3 Rope Climbs 20ft
```
