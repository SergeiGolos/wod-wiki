# 2012 CrossFit Games - Event 12 "Double Banger"

**Location:** Home Depot Center Stadium, Carson, California
**Date:** July 15, 2012 (Day 4 - Sunday)

## Description

Three rounds of 50 double-unders separated by hammering a heavy skid along a sliding track from three different starts — low, down, and mid banger; the field was cut to 18 after this event.

```time
50 Double Unders
Low Banger
50 Double Unders
Down Banger
50 Double Unders
Mid Banger
```

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 9-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Double-under, banger
- **Intensity:** Very high

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
