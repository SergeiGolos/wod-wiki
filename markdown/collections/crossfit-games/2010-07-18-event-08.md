# 2010 CrossFit Games - Event 8 "The Final 2"

**Location:** Home Depot Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 18, 2010 (Day 3 - Sunday)

## Description

Second leg of the three-part Final: three rounds of thirty toes-to-bars and twenty-one ground-to-overheads against a 7-minute cap.

```time
(3)
  30 Toes to Bar
  21 Ground to Overheads 95lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Ground to Overheads |
|-------|---------------------|
| Men | 95 lb |
| Women | 65 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Short, 7-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Toes-to-bar, ground-to-overhead
- **Intensity:** Very high

## Collectible Metrics
