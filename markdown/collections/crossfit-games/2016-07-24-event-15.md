# 2016 CrossFit Games - Event 15 "Redemption"

**Location:** StubHub Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 24, 2016 (Day 5 - Sunday)

## Description

Descending pegboard ascents and thrusters: 3 and 21, then 2 and 15, then 1 and 9. Pegboards debuted the previous year; most athletes failed them then, hence the name.

```time
3 Pegboard Ascents
21 Thrusters 135lb
2 Pegboard Ascents
15 Thrusters 135lb
1 Pegboard Ascent
9 Thrusters 135lb
```
