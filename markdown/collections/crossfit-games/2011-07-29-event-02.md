# 2011 CrossFit Games - Event 2 "Skills 1"

**Location:** Home Depot Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 29, 2011 (Day 1 - Friday)

## Description

Three ranked max-effort tests summed by placement: a max L-sit hold for time, the best of two softball throws for distance, and a handstand walk for distance.

```time
1 Max Lsit Hold

```

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Skill
- **Time Domain:** Short, three discrete tests
- **Primary Movements:** L-sit hold, softball throw, handstand walk
- **Intensity:** Maximal effort
- **Skill Level:** Advanced

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Longest L-sit hold
- **Distance:** Best softball throw of two attempts
- **Distance:** Handstand walk distance
