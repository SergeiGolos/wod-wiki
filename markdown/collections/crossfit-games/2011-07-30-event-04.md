# 2011 CrossFit Games - Event 4 "Triplet Sprint"

**Location:** Home Depot Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 30, 2011 (Day 2 - Saturday)

## Description

Four rounds of muscle-ups, 245-lb deadlifts, and GHD sit-ups, with the closing sprint growing from 50 to 200 yards each round.

```time
5 Muscle Ups
10 Deadlifts 245lb
15 GHD Situps
50yd Sprint
5 Muscle Ups
10 Deadlifts 245lb
15 GHD Situps
100yd Sprint
5 Muscle Ups
10 Deadlifts 245lb
15 GHD Situps
150yd Sprint
5 Muscle Ups
10 Deadlifts 245lb
15 GHD Situps
200yd Sprint
```

## Scaling

| Level | Deadlift |
|-------|----------|
| Men | 245 lb |
| Women | 165 lb |

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Long, no time cap
