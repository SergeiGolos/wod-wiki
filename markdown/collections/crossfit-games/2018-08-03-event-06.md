# 2018 CrossFit Games - Event 6 "Clean and Jerk Speed Ladder"

**Location:** Alliant Energy Center, Madison, Wisconsin
**Date:** August 3, 2018 (Day 2 - Friday)

## Description

Race through progressively heavier clean and jerks across three rounds. Quarterfinal: 5 bars, 1-minute cap, top 20 advance. Semifinal: 5 heavier bars, 2-minute cap, top 5 advance. Final: 5 heavier bars, 3-minute cap. Ranked by time through each ladder or heaviest successful lift.
