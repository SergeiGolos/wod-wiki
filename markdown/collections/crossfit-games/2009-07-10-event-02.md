# 2009 CrossFit Games - Event 2 "Deadlift"

**Location:** The Ranch, Aromas, California
**Date:** July 10, 2009 (Day 1 - Friday)

## Description

A deadlift ladder with one successful lift every 30 seconds, bars increasing 10 lb per interval — men from 315 lb up to 505 lb, women from 185 lb up to 375 lb — until a failed lift or the top of the ladder.

```time
1 Deadlift ?lb
```

## Scaling

| Level | Deadlift |
|-------|----------|
| Men | Start 315 lb, +10 lb every 30 s, to 505 lb |
| Women | Start 185 lb, +10 lb every 30 s, to 375 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Weightlifting
- **Time Domain:** Up to 10 minutes (20 bars at 30-second intervals)
- **Primary Movements:** Deadlift
- **Intensity:** Maximal strength

## Collectible Metrics

- **Weight:** Heaviest successful deadlift
