# 2015 CrossFit Games - Event 10 "Triangle Couplet"

**Location:** StubHub Center, Carson, California
**Date:** July 25, 2015 (Day 3 - Saturday)

## Description

15-10-6 reps for time of thrusters and bar muscle-ups — triangular numbers, hence the name. The barbell advances to a new mat after each round.

```time
(15-10-6)
Thrusters 165lb
Bar Muscle Ups
```

## Scaling

| Level | Thrusters |
|-------|-----------|
| Men | 165 lb |
| Women | 115 lb |

## Breakdown

- **Modality:** Mixed modal
- **Time Domain:** Moderate, 10-minute cap
- **Primary Movements:** Thruster, bar muscle-up
- **Intensity:** Very high
- **Skill Level:** Advanced

## Collectible Metrics

- **Time:** Total time to complete
