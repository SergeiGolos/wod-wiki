# HYROX — The Race Card
**Format:** 8 × (1 km run → workout station) — 8 km of running plus 8 stations, in a fixed order, on one running clock. Everyone runs 1 km before every station; divisions differ only in weights and team structure.
**Source:** HYROX Singles Rulebook, Season 26/27 (hyrox.com/rulebook)
## The Race
```time
1km Run
1000m Ski Erg
1km Run
```

## Divisions
- **Open** — the standard division, above weights.
- **Pro** — heavier loads (Men Pro / Women Pro columns); required for Elite 15 qualification paths.
- **Doubles** — pairs; Open weights and reps are split between partners at each station, both athletes run all 8 km.
- **Relay** — teams of four, each athlete completes two run+station pairs.
- **Age groups** — 16-24, then five-year bands (25-29 … 55-59, 60-64, … 85-89). Athletes 60+ may compete in Pro divisions at regular races.
## Breakdown
- **Modality:** Mixed modal — monostructural running base with erg, sled, carrying, and gymnastics-strength stations
- **Time domain:** ~60-150 minutes for most finishers; no hard time cut for regular divisions (elite points scoring has a maximum-time threshold)
- **Primary demands:** aerobic capacity under load, grip endurance, hip and leg strength for sled and lunges
- **Transitions:** the ROXzone between station and run is where races are won or lost; practice getting on and off equipment fast
## Collectible Metrics
- **Time:** total race time on the running clock (the sport's single ranking metric)
- **Splits:** per-station and per-run splits from the timing chip
