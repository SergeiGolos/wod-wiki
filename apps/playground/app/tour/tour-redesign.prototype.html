Scope note (deliberate)

          
Static design prototype: native HTML/CSS/JS, zero requests, zero persistence, no CDN. The clock is an in-memory simulation — it does not execute the real engine (packages/wql, ScriptRuntime) and never saves to a journal or casts to a device. Metrics shown are limited to the recorded-fact vocabulary; invented fields (e.g. heart rate) are omitted, not faked. Invalid legacy syntax from the old prototype ({tag:…}, since:, ```wql, source:all) is deliberately absent. Multi-week history exists only as an explicitly opt-in, clearly badged SAMPLE dataset.

          
Design verdict

          
Ship Variant A — the continuous walkthrough keeps demo context visible at every beat and matches the existing scroll-caption story. B is the strongest power-user surface and a natural future home for the studio; its stage-context column is the pattern worth reusing. C is the most distinctive but the most layout-fragile; steal its margin annotations for the journal empty state. All three share one state machine, so the choice is purely presentational.
